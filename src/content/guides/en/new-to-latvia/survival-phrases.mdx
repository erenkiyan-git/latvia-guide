You can survive in Riga using only English, but starting every interaction with "Labdien" (Good day) and ending with "Paldies" (Thank you) will completely change how locals treat you.
</QuickAnswer>

## The "Magic" Words

Latvians are fiercely proud of their language. Just attempting to speak these few words shows immense respect.

### Greetings
* **Sveiki** (Svey-kee) – Hello! (Can be used at any time of day).
* **Labrīt** (Lahb-reet) – Good morning.
* **Labdien** (Lahb-dyen) – Good day. (The standard formal greeting).
* **Labvakar** (Lahb-vah-kar) – Good evening.

### Politeness
* **Paldies** (Pahl-dyess) – Thank you.
* **Lūdzu** (Loo-dzu) – Please / You're welcome / Here you go. (It is a very versatile word).
* **Jā** (Yah) – Yes.
* **Nē** (Nay) – No.
* **Atvainojiet** (Aht-vye-no-yet) – Excuse me / I'm sorry. (Say this if you bump into someone).

## In the Supermarket
Cashiers in supermarkets will usually say two things to you:
* **"Vai jums ir klienta karte?"** (Do you have a loyalty card?) -> Reply with "Jā" or "Nē".
* **"Vai jums vajag maisiņu?"** (Do you need a bag?) -> Reply with "Jā, lūdzu" or "Nē, paldies".

## Saying Goodbye
* **Uz redzēšanos** (Ooz red-zay-sha-nos) – Goodbye (Formal).
* **Visu labu** (Vee-su lah-boo) – All the best.
* **Atā** (Ah-tah) – Bye! (Informal, with friends).

*Tip: Don't assume people speak Russian. Always start with Latvian, and if they switch to English, follow their lead.*

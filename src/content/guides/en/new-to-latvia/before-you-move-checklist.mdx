<QuickAnswer>
You must prepare all your official documents (Apostilled and translated) before leaving your home country. Once you arrive in Latvia, getting home documents verified is extremely difficult and expensive.
</QuickAnswer>

## 3 Months Before Moving

<StepList>
  <StepListItem title="Apostille Everything">
    Your home country's university diplomas, birth certificates, and marriage certificates are useless in Latvia unless they have an [Apostille or Legalisation stamp](../documents/apostille-legalisation). You MUST get this done by your home government before you leave.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Police Clearance Certificate">
    If required for your visa, order your Criminal Record Check. It usually takes weeks to process in your home country, and it must also be Apostilled.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Book Temporary Housing">
    Do not rent an apartment long-term before seeing it in person. Book an Airbnb, hostel, or hotel for your first 2-3 weeks in Riga.
  </StepListItem>
</StepList>

## 1 Month Before Moving

<StepList>
  <StepListItem title="Translations">
    If your Apostilled documents are not in English or Latvian, get them translated by a certified sworn translator.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Health Insurance">
    If you are a non-EU citizen, buy your mandatory Latvian health insurance policy online (BTA, Balta) for the visa process.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Unlock Your Phone">
    Make sure your smartphone is carrier-unlocked so you can insert a Latvian prepaid SIM card the moment you land at the airport.
  </StepListItem>
</StepList>

## 1 Week Before Moving

<StepList>
  <StepListItem title="Print Your Documents">
    Latvian bureaucrats love paper. Print physical copies of your employment contract, university acceptance letter, insurance policy, and passport copies.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Download the Apps">
    Download the Bolt app (for taxis from the airport) and Mobilly (for public transport).
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Pack for the Weather">
    If you are arriving between October and April, pack waterproof shoes and a heavy coat. You will need them immediately.
  </StepListItem>
</StepList>

<WarningBox>
**Medicines:** If you take prescription medication, ask your doctor for a 3-month supply and bring a doctor's note (in English). It will take you a few weeks to register with a local Latvian doctor and get access to local prescriptions.
</WarningBox>

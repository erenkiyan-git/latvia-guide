Welcome to Latvia! Your first day will be busy, but here is a simple breakdown of what to prioritize to make your arrival as smooth as possible.

## 1. Get Connected (SIM Card)

You will need internet access immediately to navigate the city, order a taxi, or contact your landlord. 

You can buy a pre-paid SIM card (Zelta Zivtiņa, LMT Karte, or Bite) at:
*   **Narvesen kiosks:** Found everywhere, including the airport arrivals hall.
*   **Supermarkets:** Rimi, Maxima.

*Cost: Usually around €3 - €5 for a starter pack with data.*

<WarningBox>
  Do not throw away the plastic card your SIM comes in! It contains your PIN and PUK codes, which you will need if you restart your phone.
</WarningBox>

## 2. Get from the Airport to the City

The Riga International Airport (RIX) is very close to the city center (about 10 km).

*   **Bus 22:** The cheapest and most authentic way. The bus stop is right outside the terminal. You can buy a ticket at the Narvesen kiosk inside the terminal or from the ticket machine at the bus stop. It takes about 30 minutes to the center.
*   **Taxi (Bolt / Forus):** Download the Bolt or Forus app. A ride to the center usually costs between €15 and €25 depending on demand. 
*   **Baltic Taxi / Red Cab:** Official airport taxis waiting outside. These are more expensive (usually €25 - €35).

## 3. Get Cash (Optional, but useful)

Latvia is highly digital and almost everywhere accepts card payments (Visa/Mastercard). However, it is useful to have €20 - €50 in cash for small purchases, markets, or in case your foreign card has issues.

*   Use an ATM at the airport or in any shopping mall. 
*   Avoid "Euronet" ATMs if possible, as they often have higher fees and bad exchange rates compared to local bank ATMs (Swedbank, SEB, Citadele, Luminor).

## 4. Check into Your Accommodation

Make sure you have arranged a meeting time with your landlord, student hostel, or hotel. If you are renting an apartment, remember to check that everything matches the contract before signing or paying the deposit.

## Next Steps

Once you have a SIM card, cash, and have dropped off your bags, you are ready to explore! The next big steps will be getting your public transport card (e-talons) and figuring out where to buy groceries.

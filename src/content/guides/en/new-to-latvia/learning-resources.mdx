<QuickAnswer>
The Latvian government heavily subsidizes language learning. You can access excellent free online platforms like E-laipa, and occasionally sign up for free in-person courses funded by the municipality or the EU.
</QuickAnswer>

## 1. Free Online Tools

The best way to start before you arrive is using the state-funded digital tools.
* **E-laipa (elaipa.lv):** This is the absolute best free resource. It is a comprehensive, interactive online course developed by the Latvian Language Agency. It takes you step-by-step from A1 to B2 level with audio, exercises, and grammar explanations.
* **Mondly / Drops:** While Duolingo does not offer Latvian, apps like Mondly and Drops do have Latvian modules. They are good for building vocabulary.
* **LSM.lv (Easy Latvian):** The national broadcaster (LSM) has a section where they publish news articles in "Easy Latvian" (Vieglais valoda). You can read and listen to the audio at the same time.

## 2. Free In-Person Courses

Because the government wants foreigners to integrate, they frequently offer free physical language courses.
* **Riga Municipality Courses:** Twice a year (usually in spring and autumn), the Riga City Council announces free Latvian courses for foreigners legally living in Riga. You must follow their Facebook page or website closely, as the spots fill up in literal minutes.
* **NVA (State Employment Agency):** If you are officially unemployed, or sometimes if you are employed but want to improve your skills, the NVA offers vouchers to take free intensive Latvian courses. 
* **Integration Centers:** Organizations like *Patvērums Drošā Māja* often run free EU-funded integration and language courses for third-country nationals.

<FAQ title="Is Latvian difficult to learn?">
Yes, it is considered one of the harder European languages for English speakers to master due to its complex grammar (declensions and cases). However, because it is entirely phonetic, reading and pronouncing it is actually much easier than English!
</FAQ>

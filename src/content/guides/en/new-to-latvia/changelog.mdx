## How we verify facts

We know how stressful moving to a new country is. We operate on a strict **"No Invented Facts"** rule.

1. **Official Sources Only:** When writing about visa requirements, taxes, or employment laws, we only use data from PMLP, VID, NVA, or CSDD. We never guess based on forum rumors.
2. **The Review Interval:** Every guide has a hidden `reviewIntervalMonths` tag. Complex legal guides are scheduled for review every 6 months to catch changes in laws (like the annual minimum wage threshold update in April).
3. **The Trust Badge:** If a page has a green "Verified" badge, it means the facts on that page were checked against the cited official sources on the `lastChecked` date.

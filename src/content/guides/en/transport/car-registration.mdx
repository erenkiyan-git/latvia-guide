<QuickAnswer>
Every car in Latvia must pass an annual technical inspection (TA) and have mandatory third-party liability insurance (OCTA). Buying a used car is handled through the e-CSDD digital platform.
</QuickAnswer>

## Buying a Used Car

If you buy a used car in Latvia (e.g., from a private seller on SS.com), the transfer of ownership is incredibly fast and entirely digital.

<StepList>
  <StepListItem title="The Seller initiates the transfer">
    The seller logs into the e-CSDD portal and marks the vehicle as "sold" to you (they will need your personal code).
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="You accept the transfer">
    You log into e-CSDD, confirm you are the buyer, and pay the state registration fee (around 30 EUR).
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Get the new registration card">
    The car is now legally yours. You drive to the nearest CSDD office, hand them the old registration document, and they print a new one with your name on it in 5 minutes.
  </StepListItem>
</StepList>

## Mandatory Insurance (OCTA)

It is strictly illegal to drive a car in Latvia without active OCTA (*Obligātā civiltiesiskā transportlīdzekļu apdrošināšana*).
* **What it is:** Basic third-party liability insurance. It covers the damage you cause to other people's cars or property if you cause an accident. It does NOT pay to fix your own car.
* **How to get it:** You buy it online (e.g., on octa.lv or directly from insurers like Balta, BTA, Swedbank). You can buy coverage for 1, 3, 6, or 12 months.
* **Digital Check:** You do not need a paper sticker or certificate. The police use automatic license plate-reading cameras to check if your OCTA is valid.

## Optional Insurance (KASKO)
If you want insurance that pays to fix *your* car if you crash it into a tree, or covers theft and vandalism, you must buy a KASKO policy. This is optional but highly recommended for newer cars.

## Technical Inspection (Tehniskā Apskate / TA)
Every car in Latvia must pass a strict government mechanical inspection. 
* **Frequency:** For older cars, it must be done every year. For brand-new cars, the first inspection is after 2 years.
* **The Process:** You drive to a CSDD inspection station. Mechanics test your brakes, emissions, suspension, and lights.
* **The Result:** If you pass, you get a new sticker on your windshield. If you fail, you receive a "Sick Leave" (Slimības lapa) for the car, giving you 30 days to fix the specific mechanical issues and return for a re-test.

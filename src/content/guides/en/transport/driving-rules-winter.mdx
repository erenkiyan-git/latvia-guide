## General Driving Rules

* **Headlights:** Your low-beam headlights (or daytime running lights) MUST be turned on at all times, day or night, summer or winter.
* **Speed Limits:** The standard limit is **50 km/h** in urban areas, **90 km/h** outside cities, and up to 100 or 110 km/h on specific modern highways in the summer.
* **Speed Cameras:** Latvia uses hundreds of stationary speed cameras (Fotoradari), as well as unmarked police cars and new "average speed control" zones (where cameras measure how fast you travel between two points over several kilometers). Do not speed.
* **Zero Tolerance for Alcohol:** The legal blood alcohol limit is 0.5 promilles (0.2 for drivers with less than 2 years of experience). If you are caught driving drunk, you face massive fines, confiscation of your car, and potentially criminal charges.

## Winter Tyres (Ziemas riepas)

This is the most important rule of Latvian winters:
* **The Law:** It is strictly mandatory for all cars to be equipped with winter tyres from **December 1 to March 1**. 
* **The Tread:** The minimum allowed tread depth for winter tyres is 4 mm. The police conduct random checks on the roads in winter; if your tyres are bald, they will confiscate your license plates immediately.
* **Studded Tyres (Radžu riepas):** Allowed only from October 1 to May 1.

## Parking in Riga (Rīgas Satiksme)

Parking in the center of Riga is managed by the city (Rīgas Satiksme) and is very expensive.
* **Zones:** The city is divided into zones (A, B, C, D, R, V). Zone R (Vecrīga/Old Town) is the most expensive, costing up to 8 EUR per hour. Zone D is the cheapest.
* **How to pay:** Do not use the physical parking meters. Download the **Mobilly app** or **Rīgas Satiksme app**. You select your zone, press "Start", and press "Stop" when you leave.
* **Private Lots:** You will also see gravel parking lots managed by private companies (EuroPark, CityParks). You must use their specific apps or meters.

<WarningBox>
**The EuroPark Trap:** If you park in a private EuroPark lot, make sure you enter your license plate into their app correctly and start the timer immediately. Their cameras automatically scan plates at the entrance, and they issue 30 EUR penalty invoices mercilessly for even a 5-minute mistake.
</WarningBox>

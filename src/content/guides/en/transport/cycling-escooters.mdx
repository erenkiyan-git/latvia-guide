<QuickAnswer>
Riga is very flat, making it great for cycling, though dedicated bike lanes are still being built. You must wear a helmet if you ride an e-scooter, and you must park scooters in designated zones.
</QuickAnswer>

## E-Scooters (Elektriskie skrejriteņi)

Rental e-scooters (Bolt, Tuul, Fiqsy) are everywhere in Riga from April to November. They are locked away during the snowy winter months.

**The Strict New Rules (2024+):**
The government has cracked down heavily on e-scooter accidents. You must follow these laws, or the police will fine you:
1. **Age and License:** You must be at least 14 years old. If you are under 18, you must have a bicycle license to ride an e-scooter.
2. **Speed Limit:** The maximum allowed speed is 25 km/h. 
3. **No Passengers:** Riding two people on one scooter is strictly illegal and highly dangerous.
4. **Parking:** In the center of Riga, you can no longer leave a scooter anywhere you want. You must park it in a designated "Scooter Parking Zone" (marked on the ground and in the app). If you leave it in the middle of the sidewalk, the app will fine you.
5. **Helmets:** The laws around mandatory helmets for adults are currently tightening; check the app warnings before you ride.

## Cycling in Riga

* **Infrastructure:** Riga is slowly improving. There are excellent dedicated bike paths connecting the center to Imanta, Mežaparks, and Vecāķi (the beach). However, in the very center, you will often have to share the road with cars or the sidewalk with pedestrians.
* **Rules:** If there is a bike lane, you must use it. If there isn't, you can ride on the sidewalk, but pedestrians **always** have the right of way. You must ride at a walking pace when near pedestrians.
* **Security:** Bicycle theft is extremely common. Buy a thick, heavy U-lock (like a Kryptonite lock). Never leave your bike locked outside overnight with just a thin cable lock; it will be stolen.

<FAQ title="Where can I rent a bike?">
If you don't want to buy one, "Nextbike" operates a dock-based bike-sharing system in Riga. You unlock them using a mobile app.
</FAQ>

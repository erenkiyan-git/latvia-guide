<QuickAnswer>
The cheapest way is Bus No. 22 (around 2 EUR, takes 30-40 minutes). The fastest way is a Bolt taxi (around 15-20 EUR, takes 15-20 minutes). 
</QuickAnswer>

## 1. Bus No. 22 (The Cheap Way)
Riga International Airport (RIX) is very close to the city center. The most popular public transport option is Bus No. 22.
* **Where to find it:** Walk out of the arrivals terminal, cross the small parking lot, and you will see the bus stop.
* **Tickets:** You can buy a ticket from the ticket machine at the bus stop, at the Narvesen kiosk inside the terminal, or using the Mobilly or Rīgas Satiksme mobile apps.
* **The Route:** It goes directly to the Old Town (stop: *11. novembra krastmala* or *Autoosta*) and the Central Railway Station (*Centrālā stacija*).

## 2. Bolt / Taxi (The Fast Way)
If you have heavy luggage, ordering a ride-hailing app is the best option.
* **Bolt:** Download the Bolt app using the airport's free Wi-Fi. The pickup point is usually just outside the arrivals door. The ride to the center costs 15 to 25 EUR depending on demand.
* **Official Taxis:** You will see Baltic Taxi (green cars) and Red Cab (red/white cars) waiting outside. They have a flat-rate voucher system to the city center, but they are generally more expensive than Bolt.

<WarningBox>
**Do not accept rides from unofficial drivers.** Occasionally, men will stand near the arrivals door saying "Taxi? Taxi?". Ignore them. They are unregulated and will overcharge you massively. Always use an app or an official branded taxi from the designated lane.
</WarningBox>

## 3. The Lux Express Shuttle
There is a dedicated shuttle bus (Lux Express) that runs between the airport and the city center. It is slightly more expensive than the public bus (around 8 EUR) but offers more comfortable seating and luggage space. You can buy tickets online or directly from the driver.

<QuickAnswer>
If you hold a non-EU driving licence and stay in Latvia for more than one year, your licence becomes invalid. You must pass a medical exam and a driving test to get a Latvian licence.
</QuickAnswer>

## The One-Year Rule

If you move to Latvia from outside the EU/EEA (e.g., from the USA, India, Turkey, or the UK), you can legally drive using your home country's driving licence for **up to one year** from the date you enter Latvia.

* **After 1 year:** Your foreign licence is no longer valid for driving in Latvia. If the police stop you, you will be fined for driving without a valid license.
* **The Solution:** You must exchange your foreign licence for a Latvian one at the Road Traffic Safety Directorate (CSDD).

## How to Exchange a Non-EU Licence

Unfortunately, you cannot simply hand over your old card and get a new one. You must prove you can drive according to European standards.

1. **Medical Check:** You must visit a local doctor or a dedicated medical commission to get a driver's medical certificate. They check your eyesight and general health. The certificate is sent digitally to the CSDD database.
2. **First Aid Course:** You may need to prove you have completed a first aid course.
3. **The Driving Test:** You do not need to take the written theory test, but you **MUST pass the practical driving test** in city traffic with a CSDD examiner. 
4. **The Exchange:** Once you pass, you surrender your non-EU licence to CSDD, and they print you a Latvian EU licence.

<WarningBox>
**Do not wait until month 11!** The waiting lists for the CSDD practical driving test can be several weeks long. Furthermore, passing the strict European driving test on the first try is notoriously difficult for experienced drivers from outside Europe who have developed "bad habits". Start the process early.
</WarningBox>

## EU/EEA Citizens
If you hold a valid driving licence issued by another EU/EEA member state, it is valid in Latvia until its expiry date. You do not need to exchange it or take any tests.

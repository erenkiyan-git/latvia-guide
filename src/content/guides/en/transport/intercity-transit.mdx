<QuickAnswer>
Latvia has a great and affordable network of intercity trains (Vivi) and regional buses. You can buy almost all domestic tickets using the Mobilly app or at the station.
</QuickAnswer>

## Trains (Vivi)
The train network, operated by *Vivi* (formerly Pasažieru vilciens), connects Riga to major regional hubs like Jūrmala, Jelgava, Tukums, Ogre, Sigulda, and Daugavpils.
* **The Trains:** Latvia is currently upgrading its fleet. You will see both the new, modern electric trains (made by Škoda) and the older, noisier Soviet-era trains. 
* **Tickets:** Buy tickets via the **Vivi app**, the **Mobilly app**, or at the ticket counter in the station. If you buy a ticket from the conductor on the train, you will pay an extra surcharge.
* **Bicycles:** You can take your bike on the train, but you must buy a special "Baggage ticket" (Bagāžas biļete).

## Regional Buses
For destinations not covered by the train network (like Liepāja, Ventspils, or Kuldīga), or for faster direct routes, regional buses are the best option.
* **The Hub:** Almost all intercity buses leave from the **Riga International Bus Station** (Rīgas Starptautiskā autoosta), located right next to the Central Market.
* **Tickets:** You can buy tickets at the station, on the bus, or online at **bezrindas.lv** or **1188.lv**. 

## Planning Your Journey
The best website to check all schedules (both trains and buses combined) is **1188.lv** (specifically their transport section). It will show you the fastest route, the price, and the exact platform your bus leaves from.

## International Travel
If you want to travel to Estonia (Tallinn) or Lithuania (Vilnius):
* **Lux Express and Ecolines** offer highly comfortable, direct coach services with Wi-Fi and free coffee.
* **Trains:** A new direct train route now connects Riga to Vilnius (operated by LTG Link) and Riga to Tartu/Tallinn.

<QuickAnswer>
Everyone in Latvia uses the Bolt app. It is safe, cheap, and you know the price upfront. Uber does not operate in Latvia.
</QuickAnswer>

## The Ride-Hailing Apps

1. **Bolt:** This Estonian company dominates the Baltic market. You can use it to order taxis, premium cars, large vans, or just standard budget rides. You link your bank card to the app, and the payment is automatic.
2. **Forus:** A slightly newer competitor to Bolt. It is worth having both apps on your phone to compare prices during peak surge hours.
3. **Yandex Go:** *Warning:* While previously popular, the Latvian government has repeatedly blocked/banned Yandex due to data security concerns regarding its Russian ownership. Do not rely on it.

*Note: Uber does not exist in Latvia.*

## Traditional Taxis

You will see traditional taxis (with a yellow light on the roof) waiting at taxi ranks near hotels, the airport, and the Old Town.
* **The Good:** Companies like *Baltic Taxi* (green cars) and *Red Cab* (red and white) are reputable, safe, and use strict meters.
* **The Bad:** Unbranded or "independent" taxis waiting near the Old Town at 3:00 AM. 

<WarningBox>
**The "Nightclub Taxi" Scam:** Never get into an unbranded taxi waiting outside a nightclub without explicitly agreeing on the price first. They will turn on a "special night tariff" meter and demand 50 EUR for a 5-minute drive. If you refuse to pay, they will lock the doors. Always use Bolt when going home late at night.
</WarningBox>

<QuickAnswer>
If there are no injuries and only two cars are involved, you do not need the police; you just fill out an "Agreed Statement of Facts" form. If anyone is hurt or if three cars are involved, you must call 110 immediately.
</QuickAnswer>

## Minor Accidents (No Police Needed)

If you have a minor crash (fender bender), you can use a **Saskaņotais paziņojums** (Agreed Statement of Facts). This saves you from waiting hours for the police.

**You can use this form ONLY if ALL of these conditions are met:**
1. Only TWO vehicles are involved.
2. Nobody is injured.
3. No third-party property is damaged (e.g., you didn't crash into a fence or a streetlamp).
4. Both cars can still physically drive away.
5. Both drivers agree on whose fault it was.

**What to do:**
* Always keep a paper copy of the *Saskaņotais paziņojums* in your glovebox (you get them from your insurance company or gas stations).
* You can also use the official "LTAB OCTA" mobile app to fill it out digitally.
* Both drivers fill it out, draw a diagram of the crash, sign it, and then you notify your insurance companies.

## Major Accidents (Call the Police: 110)

You **MUST** call the State Police (110) or Emergency Services (112) if:
* Anyone is injured, even slightly.
* More than two cars are involved.
* A car cannot be driven away.
* You damaged public or private property (a traffic sign, a parked car where the owner is not there).
* The other driver refuses to sign the Agreed Statement or appears to be drunk.

<StepList>
  <StepListItem title="Turn on Hazard Lights">
    Immediately turn on your hazard lights and set up the red warning triangle 50 meters behind your car. Put on your reflective vest before stepping onto the road.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Do Not Move the Cars">
    Do not move the vehicles until the police arrive and take photos, unless the cars are completely blocking a major highway and causing immense danger.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Call 110">
    Tell the operator your location and if an ambulance is needed.
  </StepListItem>
</StepList>

<WarningBox>
**Hit and Run:** If you scrape a parked car in a supermarket parking lot, do not drive away! Leaving the scene of an accident is a severe traffic offense in Latvia and can result in the loss of your driving licence. If you cannot find the owner, call the police to report it.
</WarningBox>

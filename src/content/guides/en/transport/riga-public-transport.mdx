Riga has a well-connected network of buses, trams, and trolleybuses operated by Rīgas Satiksme.

## E-talons (Tickets)
You need a ticket before you travel. You cannot buy a ticket from the driver anymore.
* **Yellow e-talons:** A cardboard ticket loaded with a specific number of rides or days. Buy them at Narvesen kiosks, Rimi supermarkets, or ticket machines at major stops.
* **Blue e-talons:** A personalized plastic card (requires a photo and application). Useful if you have a student or resident discount.
* **Mobile App:** You can download the "Rīgas Satiksme" app or "Mobilly" to buy and scan code tickets using your smartphone.

## How to ride
When you board the vehicle, immediately tap your e-talons on the yellow card reader, or scan the QR code using your app.

<WarningBox>
  Ticket inspectors randomly board vehicles to check tickets. If you have not registered your ticket immediately upon boarding, you will be fined (starting from €15).
</WarningBox>

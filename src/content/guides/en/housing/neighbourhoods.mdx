<QuickAnswer>
The City Center is beautiful but expensive. Students love Āgenskalns and Teika. Purvciems and Imanta offer cheaper Soviet-era apartments with great transport links.
</QuickAnswer>

## 1. The Premium Areas
* **Vecrīga (Old Town):** Beautiful cobblestone streets, but very noisy on weekends due to bars, and supermarkets are scarce. Expensive.
* **Klusais Centrs (Quiet Centre):** Stunning Art Nouveau architecture, embassies, and fine dining. The most expensive and prestigious area to live in Riga.
* **Centrs (The City Center):** Busy, convenient, full of cafes and shops. Great if you want to walk everywhere, but air quality and noise can be issues on main streets (like Čaka iela).

## 2. The Hip & Student-Friendly Areas
* **Āgenskalns:** Located across the river (Pārdaugava). Famous for its old wooden houses, large parks, the renovated Āgenskalns Market, and a vibrant, artsy community. Very popular with students (close to RTU and RSU).
* **Teika:** A quiet, green, and safe neighbourhood slightly east of the center. Built largely during the first Latvian independence. Popular with families and students.
* **Grīziņkalns:** Historically a rougher working-class area, but rapidly gentrifying. Close to the center, lots of renovated wooden buildings, and cheaper rent.

## 3. The Soviet-Era "Sleeping Districts"
These areas are dominated by massive concrete apartment blocks built during the Soviet occupation. They lack architectural charm but are very practical.
* **Purvciems & Pļavnieki:** Huge populations, excellent bus/trolleybus connections to the center, and massive supermarkets on every corner. Rent is much cheaper here.
* **Imanta & Zolitūde:** Located on the way to Jūrmala (the beach). Very green, with dedicated bike paths and train connections to the center. 
* **Ziepniekkalns:** Located in the south. Affordable and well-connected.

## Outside Riga
If you want to escape the city:
* **Jūrmala:** The famous beach resort town. It is very expensive (especially in summer) but a 30-minute train ride to Riga.
* **Mārupe:** A wealthy, rapidly growing suburb right next to Riga and the airport. Mostly private houses, popular with expat families. 
* **Ogre / Jelgava / Sigulda:** Smaller, quieter towns with regular train connections to Riga. Cheaper living costs and much closer to nature.

Finding the right apartment takes time. Here are the most common ways locals and expats find housing in Latvia.

## Main Real Estate Portals
* **SS.com (SS.lv):** The oldest and most popular classifieds site in Latvia. The interface is dated and mostly in Latvian/Russian, but it has the most listings. Use browser translation!
* **City24.lv:** A more modern portal, usually featuring listings from real estate agencies.
* **Inch.lv:** A newer, modern platform for renting and buying property.

## Facebook Groups
Many people find affordable apartments or rooms directly from owners on Facebook. Search for:
* "Riga apartments for rent"
* "Īre Rīgā" (Rent in Riga)

<WarningBox>
  **Scam Warning:** Never send money (deposit or first month's rent) via wire transfer before viewing the apartment in person and signing a physical contract. If a deal looks too good to be true, it probably is.
</WarningBox>

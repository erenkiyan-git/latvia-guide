<QuickAnswer>
Internet is cheap and incredibly fast in Latvia. You usually set up the internet contract yourself. Electricity is sometimes included in the main utility bill, or you must sign a contract directly with an energy provider.
</QuickAnswer>

## Home Internet

Latvia has some of the fastest and cheapest fiber-optic internet in the world.
* **The Big Providers:** Tet, Balticom, and Baltcom are the main providers of home fiber internet.
* **Mobile Routers:** If the apartment doesn't have a fiber connection, you can get a 5G/4G wireless router from mobile companies like LMT, Tele2, or Bite. You just plug it into the wall.
* **Cost:** Expect to pay around 15 to 25 EUR per month for unlimited, fast home internet.
* **Setup:** You contact the provider, sign a contract (usually 12 or 24 months), and they will mail you the router or send a technician to install it within a few days.

## Electricity (Elektrība)

Unlike water or heating, which are always managed by the building, electricity is an open market in Latvia.

**Scenario A: It's in the bill.**
Many landlords keep the electricity contract in their name. You just check the meter at the end of the month, give the reading to the landlord, and the cost is added to your monthly utility bill. 

**Scenario B: You sign the contract.**
If the landlord tells you to sign your own contract:
1. You choose a provider (Elektrum is the biggest, but Enefit and Tet also sell electricity).
2. You sign a contract online.
3. You choose between a "Fixed Rate" (the price per kWh stays the same for 12/24 months) or a "Stock Exchange Rate" (Biržas tarifs - the price fluctuates every hour based on the Nord Pool energy market). 

## Submitting Meter Readings
If your apartment has manual water or electricity meters (skaitītāji), you must look at the numbers on the dials between the **25th and 27th** of every month and submit them electronically to your building manager or electricity provider. If you forget, they will estimate your usage, which is usually more expensive.

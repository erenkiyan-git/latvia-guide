<QuickAnswer>
Always sign a "Handover Protocol" (Pieņemšanas-nodošanas akts) when receiving and returning the keys. Take photos of every scratch on day one so the landlord cannot blame you for old damage.
</QuickAnswer>

## Moving In: The Handover Protocol

When you meet the landlord to get the keys, you should sign a document called a **Pieņemšanas - nodošanas akts** (Acceptance and Handover Act). This is often an annex to your rental contract.

<StepList>
  <StepListItem title="Record the Meters">
    Write down the exact numbers on the hot water, cold water, and electricity meters on the day you move in. Both you and the landlord must sign this. You are not responsible for bills before these numbers.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Inventory the Furniture">
    The protocol should list everything in the apartment (e.g., 1 sofa, 1 washing machine, 1 fridge). Check that the appliances actually work before signing.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Document Existing Damage">
    If the sofa is stained, the wall is scratched, or a window handle is broken, write it down in the protocol. 
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Take Photos">
    Take clear photos of the entire apartment, including the meters and any existing damage, and save them.
  </StepListItem>
</StepList>

## Moving Out

When your contract ends and you are ready to leave:

<StepList>
  <StepListItem title="Clean thoroughly">
    The apartment must be returned in the same condition you received it. If you leave it dirty, the landlord can legally deduct a cleaning fee from your deposit.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Sign the final Protocol">
    Meet the landlord. Inspect the apartment together. Record the final meter readings. Both of you must sign the final handover protocol.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Cancel your Address">
    Do not forget to log into Latvija.lv and cancel your declared address (deklarēšanās) at that property.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Get your Deposit back">
    Once the final utility bill for the month arrives and is paid, the landlord should transfer the remainder of your deposit back to your bank account.
  </StepListItem>
</StepList>

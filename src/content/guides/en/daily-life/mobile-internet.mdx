<QuickAnswer>
Buy a prepaid SIM card at any Narvesen kiosk the moment you arrive. The major providers are LMT, Tele2, and Bite. Mobile data in Latvia is incredibly fast and cheap, often around 15 EUR for unlimited 5G.
</QuickAnswer>

## The Big Three Providers

Latvia has three major mobile network operators. All of them offer excellent 4G/5G coverage across the entire country, even deep in the forests.

1. **LMT (Latvijas Mobilais Telefons):** The state-backed provider. Generally considered to have the best and most expansive rural coverage.
2. **Tele2:** Very popular, strong network, often the first to roll out new commercial 5G zones.
3. **Bite:** Known for younger branding and very competitive prices. 

## Prepaid vs. Postpaid

### Prepaid SIM (Priekšapmaksas karte)
* **Best for:** Your first few weeks, or if you don't have a residence permit yet.
* **How to get it:** Walk into any Narvesen kiosk, Rimi supermarket, or gas station. Buy a starter pack for 1 to 5 EUR.
* **Brands:** Zelta Zivtiņa (Tele2), LMT Karte (LMT), and Bite prepaid.
* **No ID needed:** Unlike in Germany or Spain, you do not need to show your passport to buy and activate a prepaid SIM card in Latvia. You just put it in your phone.

### Postpaid Contract (Pēcapmaksa)
* **Best for:** Long-term residents. You get a monthly bill and can keep the same number forever.
* **How to get it:** You must go to a provider's store (in any shopping mall) with your passport and Latvian Residence Permit. 
* **The Catch:** Some providers will not give you a postpaid contract if your residence permit is valid for less than a year, or they might ask for an upfront safety deposit.

<FAQ title="Does my Latvian SIM work in Europe?">
Yes! Thanks to the EU's "Roam Like at Home" rules, if you travel from Latvia to Italy, Germany, or any other EU/EEA country, you can use your Latvian data and call minutes without extra roaming charges. (Always check your specific tariff's "EU Data Limit" before traveling).
</FAQ>

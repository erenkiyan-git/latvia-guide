<QuickAnswer>
The supermarket landscape in Latvia is dominated by Rimi and Maxima, with Lidl offering the cheapest prices. For fresh, local food, you should visit the Riga Central Market.
</QuickAnswer>

## The Major Supermarkets

1. **Rimi:** The premium, Swedish-owned supermarket chain. They have clean, modern stores, excellent fresh bakeries, and a wide variety of imported foods (vegan, gluten-free, international cuisines). They are generally the most expensive option.
2. **Maxima:** The Lithuanian-owned giant. Slightly cheaper than Rimi. 
   * *Pro Tip:* Pay attention to the "X"s. A "Maxima X" is a small neighborhood store. A "Maxima XXX" is a massive hypermarket that sells everything from food to bicycles.
3. **Lidl:** The German discounter entered Latvia recently and is incredibly popular. It is the cheapest place to buy basic groceries, but they mostly sell their own brands, so you won't find specific Latvian or international niche products here.
4. **Mego & Lats:** Smaller, local neighborhood convenience stores.

## Loyalty Cards (Klienta karte)
You absolutely must get the loyalty cards for Rimi (Mans Rimi) and Maxima (Paldies karte). 
* In Latvia, supermarkets use heavy "two-tier" pricing. An item might cost 4 EUR normally, but 2 EUR if you scan the loyalty card. You will lose a lot of money if you shop without them. 
* You can get digital versions of these cards on their mobile apps.

## Riga Central Market (Centrāltirgus)
Located in massive former Zeppelin hangars next to the Old Town, this is one of the largest markets in Europe.
* **Why go?** It is the best place to buy fresh meat, fish, seasonal berries, and vegetables directly from local farmers. 
* **Cost:** It is often cheaper than supermarkets, and the quality of local produce is much higher.
* **Note:** Bring some cash, though many vendors now accept card payments. 

## Alcohol Sales
Latvia has strict rules regarding alcohol.
* You can only buy alcohol in stores between **08:00 and 22:00**.
* At 22:01, the cash registers will legally block the sale of alcohol. Do not try to argue with the cashier.

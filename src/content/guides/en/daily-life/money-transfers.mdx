<QuickAnswer>
Latvia uses the Euro (€). If you need to send money outside the EU (e.g., to India, Turkey, or the US), do not use traditional Latvian banks. Use fintech apps like Wise or Revolut to avoid huge exchange rate markups.
</QuickAnswer>

## Everyday Payments in Latvia

Latvia is a heavily cashless society. 
* You can pay by contactless card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay almost everywhere—even for a 1 EUR coffee or at a stall in the Central Market.
* Cash is only strictly necessary if you travel deep into the countryside or buy from small independent farmers at local fairs.

## Transferring Money Within the EU (SEPA)

If you are sending Euros from your Latvian bank account to another bank account inside the European Union (or EEA), it is processed as a SEPA transfer.
* **Cost:** SEPA transfers are legally mandated to be free (or cost the same as a domestic transfer, which is usually zero).
* **Speed:** Instant SEPA transfers (Zibmaksājumi) arrive in seconds, 24/7. Most Latvian banks support this.

## Transferring Money Outside the EU

If you receive your salary in Euros but need to send money to your family in a non-EU currency (or if your parents are sending you money for university tuition from outside the EU), **be careful.**

<WarningBox>
If you use a traditional bank (like Swedbank or SEB) to wire money internationally via SWIFT, the bank will charge a flat fee (often 15-30 EUR) PLUS they will use a poor exchange rate, taking a hidden percentage of your money.
</WarningBox>

### The Best Alternatives
1. **Wise (formerly TransferWise):** The gold standard for expats. You transfer Euros to Wise locally, and they pay out the local currency in your home country using the real mid-market exchange rate.
2. **Revolut:** Offers excellent exchange rates and allows you to hold multiple currencies in one app. Very popular in Latvia.
3. **Western Union / MoneyGram:** Available in Latvia (often at post offices or specific kiosks), but their fees are significantly higher than digital apps. Use only for emergencies if the receiver does not have a bank account.

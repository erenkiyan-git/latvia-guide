<QuickAnswer>
Bringing a pet requires a microchip, rabies vaccination, and an EU Pet Passport or an official veterinary certificate from your home country. In Latvia, all dogs must be registered in the national database.
</QuickAnswer>

## Bringing your Pet to Latvia

The rules depend on where you are traveling from:

### 1. From within the EU
If you are moving from Germany or Spain, your pet simply needs a microchip, a valid rabies vaccination, and a blue **EU Pet Passport**. There are no border checks.

### 2. From outside the EU
If you are moving from a third country (like Turkey, USA, or India), the process is strict:
* **Microchip:** Must be ISO-compliant.
* **Rabies Vaccine:** Must be administered *after* the microchip is implanted.
* **Rabies Titre Test:** Required if you are traveling from a "high-risk" rabies country (e.g., Turkey, India). The blood test must show sufficient antibodies, and you must wait 3 months after the test before entering the EU.
* **Veterinary Certificate:** A government-approved vet in your home country must issue an EU Health Certificate within 10 days of your travel.

<WarningBox>
**Do not forge pet documents.** If border control suspects your pet's rabies certificate is fake, the animal will be quarantined at your expense, or in worst-case scenarios, euthanized.
</WarningBox>

## Living with a Pet in Latvia

* **Dog Registration:** By law, all dogs in Latvia must be microchipped and registered in the Agricultural Data Centre (LDC) database before they reach 4 months of age. Your vet will help you do this.
* **Leash Laws:** Dogs must be kept on a leash in all public spaces in Riga, except in specifically marked dog parks. 
* **Renting with a Pet:** Finding a rental apartment with a pet is notoriously difficult in Riga. Many landlords explicitly forbid animals. You will likely need to offer a higher security deposit to convince a landlord.
* **Ticks:** Latvia's forests are full of ticks in the spring and summer. Some carry tick-borne encephalitis and Lyme disease. You must give your dog preventative tick medicine from April to October.

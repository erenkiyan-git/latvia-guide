<QuickAnswer>
State-funded kindergartens and schools are free but teach exclusively in Latvian. International families who do not speak Latvian usually send their children to expensive private international schools in Riga.
</QuickAnswer>

## Kindergartens (Bērnudārzs)

Pre-school education is compulsory in Latvia for children aged 5 and 6. 

* **State Kindergartens:** Free of charge (you only pay a small fee for meals). However, there are massive waiting lists in Riga. You must register your child in the municipal queue the moment you declare your address. The teaching language is Latvian.
* **Private Kindergartens:** Cost around 300 to 500 EUR per month. If the municipality cannot offer your child a spot in a state kindergarten, they will "co-finance" (pay a portion of) the private kindergarten fee. 

## Primary and Secondary Schools

The school system consists of 9 years of basic education (mandatory) and 3 years of secondary education.

### State Schools
State schools are completely free. However, recent language reforms mean that all state-funded education is transitioning to be **100% in the Latvian language**. 
If you are moving to Latvia for a few years and your older children do not speak a word of Latvian, putting them in a state school will be incredibly difficult for them academically.

### International Schools
Most expats on temporary work assignments send their children to private international schools. These schools teach in English and offer IB (International Baccalaureate) or British curriculums. 
* **The main options in Riga:** International School of Riga (ISR), International School of Latvia (ISL - located in Piņķi), and Exupéry International School.
* **The catch:** They are very expensive. Tuition can range from 10,000 EUR to over 20,000 EUR per year. If you are moving to Latvia for work, you should negotiate with your employer to cover your children's tuition fees in your contract.

Latvia's banking system is modern, but opening a traditional bank account as a non-resident can involve high fees and strict background checks.

## Neo-banks (The easy way)
Most expats and students use digital banks because they are free, easy to set up from your phone, and provide an EU IBAN.
* **Revolut:** Extremely popular in Latvia.
* **Wise:** Great for international transfers.

## Traditional Banks
If you need a local bank (e.g., your employer demands it, or you want a local mortgage), the main banks are:
* **Swedbank:** The largest network of ATMs.
* **SEB:** Very popular and reliable.
* **Citadele:** Known for good digital services.
* **Luminor:** A large Baltic bank.

<WarningBox>
  If you do not have a Latvian residence permit, traditional banks may charge a "document review fee" (often €100 - €250) just to consider opening an account, and it is non-refundable even if they reject you.
</WarningBox>

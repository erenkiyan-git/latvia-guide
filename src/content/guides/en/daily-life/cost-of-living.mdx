<QuickAnswer>
Latvia is cheaper than Western Europe, but not "cheap". A single person renting a private apartment in Riga should budget around 800 - 1000 EUR per month for a comfortable, modest life.
</QuickAnswer>

## Estimated Monthly Budget (Single Person in Riga)

*These are rough estimates and can vary wildly based on your lifestyle.*

### 1. Housing (400 - 600 EUR)
* **1-Bedroom Apartment Rent:** 300 - 450 EUR (outside the center) or 450 - 600+ EUR (in the center).
* **Utilities (Winter):** 150 - 250 EUR.
* **Internet:** 15 - 20 EUR.

### 2. Groceries & Food (250 - 350 EUR)
* If you cook mostly at home and shop at Lidl or the Central Market, you can eat well on 250 EUR a month.
* **Dining out:** A business lunch in the center costs 6 - 9 EUR. A dinner at a mid-range restaurant is 20 - 35 EUR per person. A pint of local beer in a bar is 4 - 6 EUR.

### 3. Transport (30 - 50 EUR)
* **Monthly Public Transport Ticket:** 30 EUR (or 15 EUR if you are a student).
* **Bolt/Taxi:** A 15-minute ride across the city usually costs 5 - 8 EUR.

### 4. Health & Leisure (50 - 100 EUR)
* **Gym Membership:** 25 - 50 EUR (MyFitness or Lemon Gym).
* **Cinema Ticket:** 8 - 11 EUR.

## Is it cheaper outside Riga?
Yes, significantly. Renting an apartment in cities like Jelgava, Liepāja, or Valmiera is much cheaper than in Riga. However, salaries outside the capital are also much lower, and the international job market is almost non-existent.

<FAQ title="What is a 'good' salary in Riga?">
If you earn 1500 EUR Net (take-home pay) per month, you can live very comfortably as a single person in Riga. You can rent a nice apartment, eat out occasionally, and save money. If you earn under 800 EUR Net, you will need to flat-share and budget strictly.
</FAQ>

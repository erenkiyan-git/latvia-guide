<QuickAnswer>
You must sort your trash. Apartment buildings have different colored bins for general waste, paper/plastic, and glass. Latvia also has a deposit system (Taromāts) where you get 0.10 EUR back for recycling bottles.
</QuickAnswer>

## The Bin System

Every apartment building has a designated trash area (usually in the courtyard). You will find several colored containers:

1. **Green or Black Bin (Sadzīves atkritumi):** Unsorted general waste (food scraps, dirty packaging, anything that cannot be recycled).
2. **Yellow Bin (Plastmasa, Papīrs, Metāls):** Clean plastic packaging, paper, cardboard, and metal cans. 
3. **Green Bell-Shaped Bin (Stikls):** Glass bottles and jars only.

*Note: You do not need to buy special colored garbage bags in Latvia. You can use any plastic bag.*

## The Bottle Deposit System (Depozīta sistēma)

Latvia has a nationwide bottle recycling system. When you buy a drink (water, soda, beer) in a plastic bottle, glass bottle, or metal can, you are charged an extra **0.10 EUR**.

**How to get your money back:**
Look for the special "Depozīta sistēma" logo on the bottle's label. Do not crush the bottle! Take your empty bottles to a **Taromāts** (a large recycling machine found outside almost every supermarket). 
1. Feed the bottles into the machine.
2. The machine prints a paper receipt.
3. Take the receipt to the supermarket checkout, and they will deduct the amount from your grocery bill (or give you cash).

<FAQ title="What about old electronics or batteries?">
Never throw batteries in the general trash. Every supermarket has a small transparent box near the entrance specifically for dead batteries. For large electronics, you must take them to designated Eko-laukums (Eco-yards) or wait for specific collection days arranged by your building manager.
</FAQ>

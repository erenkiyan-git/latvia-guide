<QuickAnswer>
If you legally reside in Latvia (as a citizen, permanent resident, or on a temporary permit), you can invite your spouse and minor children through family reunification (*ģimenes apvienošana*). You must prove the family relationship and show you have enough income to support them.
</QuickAnswer>

## Who can apply
* Spouses of Latvian citizens or residents.
* Minor children (under 18) of Latvian citizens or residents.
* Parents of Latvian citizens (under specific conditions of dependency or age).

**Who cannot apply:** You generally cannot bring siblings, adult children, or extended family members under standard family reunification rules.

## Before you apply (Preparation timeline)
Gathering family documents takes time. Marriage certificates and birth certificates must usually be legalised (Apostille) in the country where they were issued.

## Step-by-Step Application Process

<StepList>
  <StepListItem title="Sponsor submits Invitation">
    The family member already in Latvia (the sponsor) submits an invitation request to PMLP, proving their income and the relationship.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Family member applies">
    Once the invitation is approved, the family member abroad submits their application, police clearance, and apostilled marriage/birth certificates to the Latvian embassy.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Wait for Decision">
    PMLP processes the application. The standard time is 30 days.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Travel and Collect Card">
    Upon approval, the family member gets a D-Visa to enter Latvia, provides biometrics at PMLP, and collects their residence permit card.
  </StepListItem>
</StepList>

## Fees and Processing Time
* **Processing time:** Standard 30 days. Expedited options may be available.
* **Subsistence requirements:** Check the official source: [PMLP Subsistence](https://www.pmlp.gov.lv/en). Typically a specific amount per adult and per child.
* **State Fee:** Check the official source: [PMLP State Fees](https://www.pmlp.gov.lv/en)

## After Approval: What to do next
Family members must [declare their address](./address-declaration) just like the sponsor. They should also be added to local health insurance or register with a family doctor if applicable.

## Renewal and Changes
The family member's permit is usually tied to the sponsor's permit. If the sponsor's permit expires or is cancelled, the family member's permit is also cancelled.

## Common Mistakes
* **Translating before Apostille:** Always get the Apostille on the original marriage/birth certificate *first*, and then translate the document along with the Apostille stamp.
* **Insufficient funds:** The sponsor's salary must clearly meet the threshold required to support themselves plus all invited family members.

## If your application is refused
If PMLP doubts the legitimacy of a marriage (suspecting a "marriage of convenience"), they will conduct interviews and may refuse the permit. You can appeal, but you should consult an immigration lawyer.

<FAQ title="Can my spouse work in Latvia?">
It depends on the sponsor's status. Spouses of Latvian citizens, non-citizens, permanent residents, and EU Blue Card holders generally get free access to the labor market. Spouses of standard Temporary Residence Permit holders (like students or regular workers) often have restrictions and may need their own work permit. Check the specific wording on the issued ID card.
</FAQ>

<QuickAnswer>
Every official document in Latvia comes with a state fee. Fees vary widely based on how fast you want the document processed. You must always check the official PMLP website before paying, as amounts can change.
</QuickAnswer>

<WarningBox>
**Disclaimer:** The amounts below are estimates to give you an idea of the costs. They might not reflect the exact current fees. Always check the official links provided before making any payments.
</WarningBox>

<QuickAnswer>
Once PMLP approves your application, you must book an appointment to give biometrics. Your physical card is then printed and ready to collect, usually within 10 working days.
</QuickAnswer>

## The Final Steps

Receiving the decision letter that your residence permit is "approved" is a great feeling, but you are not done until you have the physical plastic card.

1. **Travel to Latvia (if outside):** If you are abroad, you will use the approval letter to get a Type D visa from the embassy to enter Latvia.
2. **Book an Appointment:** Use the PMLP online booking system to schedule an appointment for "Residence Permits".
3. **Give Biometrics:** At the appointment, you will show your passport, provide your fingerprints, give a digital signature, and have your photo taken.
4. **Pay the Card Fee:** You must pay a fee to cover the cost of printing the physical card. You can usually pay by card at the PMLP desk.
5. **Wait for Printing:** The standard processing time to print the card is 10 working days. You can pay an extra fee to have it expedited (usually 2 working days).
6. **Collection:** You must return to PMLP to pick up the card. You will also receive an envelope with PIN codes (PIN1, PIN2, PUK) for the electronic signature features of the card. **Do not lose this envelope.**

## What does the card do?

The Residence Permit (TRP) card acts as your primary ID inside Latvia. It contains:
* Your photo and signature.
* Your Latvian Personal Code (personas kods).
* The validity dates of your permit.
* Information about your right to work (e.g., "Right to employment with a specific employer" or "Right to work 20h/week").

<FAQ title="Do I need to carry the card with me?">
Yes. Foreigners in Latvia must be able to present their identity document and proof of legal stay upon request by police or border guards. 
</FAQ>

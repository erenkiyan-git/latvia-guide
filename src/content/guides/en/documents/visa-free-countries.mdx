<QuickAnswer>
Citizens of the EU, EEA, USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Japan, and several other nations can enter Latvia visa-free for short visits (up to 90 days). You cannot work or study long-term without applying for a permit.
</QuickAnswer>

## Who can enter without a visa?

If your passport is from an EU or EEA country, you have freedom of movement.

For third-country nationals, Latvia follows the common Schengen visa policy. Citizens from many countries do not need a visa for tourism, visiting family, or short business trips.

Because the list of countries can change based on international agreements, **you must check the official Ministry of Foreign Affairs list** to confirm your status. 

<WarningBox>
Even if you are visa-free, you cannot work in Latvia (not even volunteering or unpaid work in many cases) without the correct visa or work permit.
</WarningBox>

## What you need at the border

Even if you do not need a visa, border guards can still deny you entry if you cannot prove the purpose of your trip. You should always carry:
* A passport valid for at least 3 months beyond your planned departure date.
* A return ticket.
* Proof of accommodation (hotel booking or address of the person hosting you).
* Proof of sufficient funds for your stay.
* Travel medical insurance.

## Upcoming Change: ETIAS

The European Union is implementing the European Travel Information and Authorisation System (ETIAS). 

Once active, visa-free travelers (e.g., from the US or UK) will need to fill out a quick online form and pay a small fee before traveling to Latvia or any Schengen country. Check the official EU website for the launch date.

<QuickAnswer>
Latvia offers a 1-year Digital Nomad Visa for remote workers employed by a company registered in an OECD country, or self-employed persons registered in an OECD country. You must meet a high minimum income threshold.
</QuickAnswer>

## Who can apply
* Non-EU citizens who work remotely for an employer registered in an OECD country.
* Self-employed persons whose business is registered in an OECD country.
* You must have been employed with this company (or self-employed) for at least the last 6 months.

**Who cannot apply:** You cannot apply if your employer is based in a non-OECD country. You cannot work for local Latvian companies on this visa.

## Before you apply (Preparation timeline)
You need to gather tax documents and a letter from your employer proving you are allowed to work remotely.

## Step-by-Step Application Process

<StepList>
  <StepListItem title="Prepare Documents">
    Gather your employment contract, the employer's letter, and your bank statements.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Submit Application">
    Submit your Long-Stay (Type D) Visa application to the Latvian embassy or directly to PMLP if you are already legally in Latvia.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Wait for Decision">
    PMLP processes the application within roughly 15 days.
  </StepListItem>
</StepList>

## Fees and Processing Time
* **Processing time:** Typically around 15 working days.
* **Income Threshold:** You must prove a monthly income of at least 2.5 times the average Latvian gross wage. Check the exact current figure on the official site.
* **State Fee:** Check the official source: [PMLP State Fees](https://www.pmlp.gov.lv/en).

## After Approval: What to do next
You will receive a National D-Visa. You must enter Latvia and [declare your address](./address-declaration). Since you are not entering the local labor market, you do not get a standard Residence Permit card, but a visa sticker.

## Renewal and Changes
The Digital Nomad Visa is issued for 1 year. It can be renewed for 1 more year. After 2 years, you must either leave Latvia or change to a different residence permit type (e.g., standard employment or starting a local business).

## Common Mistakes
* **Non-OECD employer:** Applying when your employer is not registered in an OECD member state.
* **Insufficient income history:** Failing to prove that you have earned the high minimum salary threshold consistently for the past 6 months.

## If your application is refused
You can appeal the decision. Make sure your income proof is crystal clear and matches your bank statements.

<FAQ title="Do I pay taxes in Latvia?">
Yes, if you spend more than 183 days in Latvia, you become a tax resident and may be liable for Personal Income Tax in Latvia. You should consult a Latvian tax advisor regarding double-taxation treaties with your home country.
</FAQ>

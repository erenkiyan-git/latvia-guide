<QuickAnswer>
If you are outside Latvia, you usually apply at the nearest Latvian Embassy. If you are already legally in Latvia (visa-free or with a visa), you can often submit documents directly to PMLP, but check restrictions carefully.
</QuickAnswer>

## Applying from Abroad (Embassy / Consulate)

If you need a visa to enter Latvia, you will start the process in your home country or country of residence.

<StepList>
  <StepListItem title="Your sponsor starts the process in Latvia">
    If you are coming for work, study, or family, your sponsor (employer, university, or spouse) must first register an "Invitation" at PMLP in Latvia. Once approved, they will give you an invitation number.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Prepare your documents">
    Collect your passport, police clearance, diplomas, etc. Make sure they are [apostilled/legalised](./apostille-legalisation) and translated if necessary.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Book an appointment">
    Contact the Latvian embassy or consulate responsible for your region. In some countries, Latvia outsources document collection to a visa center (like VFS Global).
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Submit and wait">
    Submit your documents and pay the state fee. The embassy forwards your file to PMLP in Riga for the decision.
  </StepListItem>
</StepList>

## Applying Inside Latvia (PMLP)

If you are already legally present in Latvia (e.g., you entered visa-free, or you hold a valid Schengen visa), you can sometimes submit your residence permit application directly to PMLP.

**Warning:** Submitting your documents to PMLP does **not** automatically extend your visa. If your 90-day Schengen visa expires while PMLP is still processing your application, you must leave the Schengen area or apply for a National D-Visa to bridge the gap. Do not overstay!

### E-Submission
PMLP strongly encourages submitting documents electronically. However, this usually requires a secure electronic signature (eParaksts). If you do not have one (which is common for new arrivals), you may need to submit documents by mail or through the official Latvija.lv portal if your university or employer assists you.

<FAQ title="Can I post my documents to PMLP?">
Yes, you can often send your paper documents by mail or courier to the PMLP office. However, check the current rules, as originals (like police certificates) must usually be shown in person eventually.
</FAQ>

<QuickAnswer>
You must report the loss to the State Police immediately and apply for a new card at PMLP within 30 days. Your e-Signature certificates will be suspended to prevent identity theft.
</QuickAnswer>

## 1. Prevent Identity Theft

Your Residence Permit is also your eID card, which contains your electronic signature. If someone steals it and figures out your PIN codes, they could theoretically sign legal documents or take out loans in your name.

* **Action:** Call the eID helpdesk immediately at **+371 67108787** to suspend your electronic signature certificates.

## 2. Inform the Authorities

<StepList>
  <StepListItem title="The Police (Valsts Policija)">
    If you suspect the card was stolen (not just dropped somewhere), you must go to the nearest State Police station and write a statement. They will give you a reference number.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="The Issuing Authority (PMLP)">
    You must notify the Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs (PMLP) that the document is lost. You can do this in person, by phone, or via the Latvija.lv portal (if you still have Smart-ID access). Once reported to PMLP, the card is officially invalidated in the Schengen database.
  </StepListItem>
</StepList>

## 3. Getting a New Card

By law, you must apply for a replacement document within **30 days** of losing the old one.

* Go to any PMLP office. You do not need a special reason; just tell them it was lost.
* You will have to pay the state fee for issuing a new eID card (usually around 15 EUR, or more for expedited processing).
* They will take a new photo and issue you a new card and a new set of PIN codes.

<WarningBox>
**What if you find the old card later?** Do not use it! Once a card is reported lost to PMLP, it is flagged in European border control databases. If you try to cross a border with a "found" card that was previously reported lost, border guards will confiscate it and may detain you for questioning. Hand the old card in to PMLP.
</WarningBox>

## If you are outside Latvia
If you lose your Latvian Residence Permit while on holiday in Spain, for example, you cannot board a plane back to Latvia because airlines check residence permits for non-EU citizens. You must go to the nearest Latvian Embassy or Consulate to apply for a temporary "Return Certificate" (Atgriešanās apliecība).

## The Big Split: EU vs. Third-Country

Understanding this difference will save you a lot of confusion:

1. **EU/EEA/Swiss Citizens (Registration):** You already have the right to live, work, and study in Latvia. You do not need a "permit" to be allowed in. However, if you stay longer than 90 days, you must **register** at the Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs (PMLP) to get a Latvian personal code (*personas kods*) and a registration certificate.
2. **Third-Country Nationals (Permits & Visas):** You do not automatically have the right to live or work in Latvia. You must apply for a specific legal reason (study, work, family). You will receive a **Temporary Residence Permit (TRP)** card.

## Typical Journey for a Third-Country National

If you are moving to Latvia for work or study, your journey usually follows these steps:

1. **Before applying:** Gather documents (diplomas, police clearance) and get them [Apostilled/Legalised](./apostille-legalisation) and translated.
2. **The Application:** Your sponsor (employer or university) submits an invitation. You submit your application to the Latvian embassy or PMLP.
3. **Decision & Visa:** PMLP approves the permit. If you need a visa to travel, the embassy issues a D-Visa so you can enter Latvia.
4. **After arrival:** Go to PMLP to give [biometrics](./biometrics) (fingerprints, face scan) and [collect your residence permit card](./card-collection).
5. **Settling in:** [Declare your address](./address-declaration) (deklarēšanās) and set up your [eID / e-signature](./eid-esignature).
6. **Renewal:** Apply to renew your permit 30–45 days before it expires.

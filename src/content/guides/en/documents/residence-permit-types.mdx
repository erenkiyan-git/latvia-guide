The Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs (PMLP) handles all residence permits (Uzturēšanās atļauja) in Latvia. 

## Common Types for Non-EU Citizens
1. **Student Permit:** Issued for the duration of your studies. Allows you to work up to 20 hours per week.
2. **Work Permit:** Tied to a specific employer who must sponsor you. If you change jobs, you need a new permit.
3. **Digital Nomad Visa:** A relatively new visa for remote workers employed by a company registered in an OECD country (valid for 1 year, renewable once).
4. **Family Reunification:** For spouses of Latvian citizens or permanent residents.

## EU Citizens
EU citizens do not need a visa, but if staying longer than 90 days, you must register at PMLP to get a registration certificate and a personal code (personas kods).

<WarningBox>
  Immigration laws change frequently. Always check the exact requirements, required documents, and processing times directly on the PMLP website (pmlp.gov.lv) before applying.
</WarningBox>

<QuickAnswer>
EU, EEA, and Swiss citizens can stay in Latvia visa-free. If staying longer than 90 days (or 6 months for job seekers), you must register at PMLP to get a registration certificate and personal code (personas kods).
</QuickAnswer>

## The 90-Day Rule

As a citizen of the European Union, European Economic Area (Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein), or Switzerland, you have the right to freely enter, live, work, and study in Latvia. 

You do not need a visa or a residence permit. 

However, if your stay exceeds **90 days within a 6-month period**, Latvian law requires you to register your residence with the Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs (PMLP). *Exception: If you are actively looking for a job, you can stay up to 6 months without registering.*

## Why Register?
Registration is simple and extremely useful. You will receive a Registration Certificate containing your **Latvian Personal Code (personas kods)**. Without this code, it is very difficult to open a local bank account, sign an internet contract, pay taxes, or use the medical system.

## Grounds for Registration
To register, you must fit into one of these categories:
1. **Employed or self-employed** in Latvia.
2. **Student** enrolled in an accredited educational institution in Latvia, with sufficient financial means and health insurance.
3. **Self-sufficient person** with enough funds to support yourself and comprehensive health insurance.
4. **Family member** of an EU citizen who meets the above criteria.

## The Process

<StepList>
  <StepListItem title="Prepare documents">
    Fill out the form and gather your proof of employment, study, or funds.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Submit to PMLP">
    You can submit the documents in person at a PMLP office (by appointment), by mail, or electronically if you already have a secure e-signature.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Receive the Certificate">
    PMLP processes the application quickly (often the same day or within a month depending on the queue). You will receive a paper or electronic Registration Certificate.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Declare your address">
    Within 1 month of receiving your certificate, you must [declare your living address](./address-declaration) in Latvia.
  </StepListItem>
</StepList>

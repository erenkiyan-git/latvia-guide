<QuickAnswer>
You must give biometrics (fingerprints and a digital face scan) to get a Latvian residence permit or visa. This must be done in person at an embassy or a PMLP office.
</QuickAnswer>

## The Process

To issue a secure ID card (Residence Permit or Foreigner's eID) or a visa, the Latvian government requires biometric data.

1. **Digital Photo:** The official will take a digital photo of your face using a special camera. You do not usually need to bring printed passport photos unless specifically asked (e.g., if the embassy equipment is broken).
2. **Fingerprints:** You will place your fingers on a digital scanner. 
3. **Signature:** You will sign on a digital pad. This signature will be printed on your physical card.

## When does it happen?

* **Applying at an Embassy:** You will usually give biometrics when you submit your paper application at the embassy or visa center.
* **Applying in Latvia:** If you sent your documents by mail or e-mail, you will need to book an appointment at a PMLP customer service center to give biometrics before they can print your card.

## Exemptions
Children under the age of 12 do not need to provide fingerprints, but their facial image must still be captured.

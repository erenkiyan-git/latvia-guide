<QuickAnswer>
Latvian law requires everyone to declare their official living address. You must do this within 1 month of moving into a new place or receiving your residence permit. You can declare online, in person at PMLP, or at the local municipality.
</QuickAnswer>

## Why is it important?

"Deklarēšanās" (declaring your address) connects you to your local municipality. 
* It proves you legally reside where you say you do.
* It dictates which municipality receives your income taxes.
* It gives you access to local benefits (like cheaper public transport in Riga or places in local kindergartens).
* **Crucially:** Official government mail (including fines and permit renewal notices) will be sent to this address.

## Do I need the landlord's permission?

**Yes and No.** You must have a legal basis to declare an address—usually a signed rental contract. If you have a valid rental contract, the landlord cannot forbid you from declaring your address there. The law actually *requires* you to do it. 

<WarningBox>
Never pay a landlord extra money just for the "permission" to declare your address. It is your legal right and obligation if you rent the property. Avoid renting from landlords who refuse to let you declare.
</WarningBox>

## How to Declare

<StepList>
  <StepListItem title="Online (Latvija.lv)">
    If you already have a Latvian eID card and a card reader (or eParaksts/Smart-ID), you can do this completely free and instantly on the portal latvija.lv.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="In person at the Municipality">
    You can go to the local municipality office (e.g., Riga City Council customer service). Bring your passport/ID card and your rental contract. You will have to pay a small state fee.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="In person at PMLP">
    If you are collecting your residence permit card, you can often declare your address at the exact same time at the PMLP office. Tell the official you want to do it and show your rental agreement.
  </StepListItem>
</StepList>

<FAQ title="What if I move?">
If you move to a new apartment, you have 1 month to declare the new address. Doing so automatically cancels your old address registration.
</FAQ>

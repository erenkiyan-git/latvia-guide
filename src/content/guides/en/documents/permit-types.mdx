<QuickAnswer>
A **Schengen Visa (C)** is for short visits (up to 90 days). A **National Visa (D)** is for longer stays or to enter Latvia while your permit is processed. A **Residence Permit** is a plastic ID card giving you the right to live (and sometimes work) in Latvia for a year or more.
</QuickAnswer>

## 1. Short-Stay (Schengen) Visa - Type C
This is the standard tourist or business visitor visa. 
* **Validity:** Allows you to stay in Latvia and the entire Schengen area for up to 90 days within any 180-day period.
* **Work:** You **cannot** work in Latvia on a Type C visa.
* **Who needs it:** Citizens of countries that do not have a [visa-free agreement](./visa-free-countries) with the EU.

## 2. Long-Stay (National) Visa - Type D
A Type D visa is issued by Latvia for stays exceeding 90 days. It is often a transitional document.
* **When it is used:** 
  * You have been approved for a residence permit while abroad, and you need a Type D visa simply to enter Latvia and collect your permit card.
  * You are coming for a specific short-term purpose (e.g., seasonal work, voluntary work, or study exchange) that lasts up to 1 year but does not require a full residence permit.
  * Digital nomads often get a Type D visa for their first year.
* **Mobility:** It allows you to stay in Latvia, and you can still travel to other Schengen countries for up to 90 days within 180 days.

## 3. Temporary Residence Permit (TRP)
A TRP (Latvian: *Termiņuzturēšanās atļauja*) is a physical plastic ID card (eID card).
* **When it is used:** If you plan to live in Latvia for more than a year (e.g., full degree studies, long-term employment, marriage to a local).
* **Rights:** It gives you a Latvian personal code (*personas kods*), which you need to open a bank account, use public medical services, sign contracts, and access government e-services. It dictates your right to work.
* **Validity:** Usually issued for 1 to 5 years, but must often be registered/renewed annually.

<FAQ title="If I have a Residence Permit, do I also need a Visa?">
No. If you have a valid Latvian residence permit card and a valid passport, you do not need a visa to enter Latvia or to travel within the Schengen area for up to 90 days.
</FAQ>

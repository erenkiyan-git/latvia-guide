<QuickAnswer>
No private agent can "guarantee" a Latvian visa or residence permit. Be highly suspicious of agencies asking for large sums of cash, offering fake job contracts, or telling you to lie to the embassy.
</QuickAnswer>

## Common Scams Targeting Foreigners

### 1. The "Fake Job Offer" Scam
**How it works:** An agency or "employer" online offers you a job in Latvia. They ask you to pay an "administrative fee," "document processing fee," or "visa fee" upfront via Western Union, Crypto, or bank transfer. Once you pay, they disappear, or send you a forged work contract that the embassy immediately rejects.
**The Truth:** It is illegal in Latvia for an employer to charge a candidate a fee for giving them a job. Legitimate employers pay you, not the other way around. 

### 2. The "Guaranteed Visa" Agent
**How it works:** An agent claims they have "connections inside PMLP" or the embassy and can guarantee your visa gets approved, even if you lack the funds or qualifications.
**The Truth:** Visas are evaluated by strict legal criteria. If an agent tells you to submit forged bank statements or fake diplomas, **you** are the one who will be caught. PMLP routinely verifies documents. You will face a Schengen-wide ban for document fraud.

### 3. The "Shell Company" Scam
**How it works:** You pay a local "consultant" to register a business (SIA) in your name in Latvia so you can get a business residence permit. The consultant sets up a fake company that does no actual business.
**The Truth:** PMLP and the State Revenue Service (VID) actively monitor foreign-owned companies. When renewal time comes, if your company has not generated real revenue or paid the required minimum state taxes, your permit will be cancelled.

## Red Flags Checklist
Walk away if an agent or employer:
* Asks you to pay money to secure a job offer.
* Tells you to hide information or lie during your embassy interview.
* Offers to provide a "fake" bank statement to prove your funds.
* Uses a generic email address (like `@gmail.com` or `@yahoo.com`) instead of a company domain.
* Refuses to communicate via official, traceable channels.

<WarningBox>
If you need help with your application, hire a licensed Latvian lawyer or a reputable, registered relocation agency. They will charge for their time and legal advice, but they will never "guarantee" a government decision or ask you to break the law.
</WarningBox>

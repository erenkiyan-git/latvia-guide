<QuickAnswer>
All documents submitted to PMLP or the embassy must be in Latvian, English, French, Russian, or German. If your document is in another language, it must be translated by a certified translator and bound to the original document.
</QuickAnswer>

## Accepted Languages

Latvia is quite flexible during the visa/residence permit process. The Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs (PMLP) officially accepts documents written in:
* Latvian
* English
* Russian
* French
* German

If your original document (for example, a Turkish police clearance or a Spanish university diploma) is not in one of these languages, **you must have it translated.**

## How to get a Certified Translation

You cannot translate the document yourself. The translation must be certified. 

### Option 1: Translate in Latvia (Recommended)
If you or your employer are already in Latvia, the safest option is to use a Latvian translation agency.
1. The agency translates the document.
2. A Latvian sworn notary (zvērināts notārs) certifies the translator's signature and binds the translation to the original document (or a certified copy).
This format is 100% guaranteed to be accepted by PMLP.

### Option 2: Translate abroad
If you are translating the document in your home country before going to the embassy:
1. Use an official, certified, or sworn translator in your country.
2. The translator's signature must be certified by a notary.
3. If your country is outside the EU, the notary's stamp certifying the translation might also need an [Apostille](./apostille-legalisation).

<WarningBox>
**The Apostille Rule:** If your original document requires an Apostille, you must get the Apostille **first**, and then give it to the translator. The translator must translate both the document text and the text of the Apostille stamp.
</WarningBox>

## Original vs. Copy

When submitting a translation to PMLP, the translation is usually bound (stitched or stapled with an official seal) to the original document. 

If you do not want to give PMLP your original diploma or marriage certificate forever, you must first make a **certified notary copy** of the document, and have the translation bound to the copy instead. You must still show the original to the official when submitting your file.

<QuickAnswer>
To work in Latvia as a non-EU citizen, you need a Work Residence Permit (*termiņuzturēšanās atļauja ar tiesībām uz nodarbinātību*) tied to a specific employer. The employer must sponsor you by registering a vacancy at the State Employment Agency (NVA) before inviting you.
</QuickAnswer>

## Who can apply
* Non-EU citizens who have a confirmed job offer from a Latvian company.
* Highly qualified specialists applying for the EU Blue Card.

**Who cannot apply:** You cannot get a general work permit just to look for a job. You must have a specific employer ready to sponsor you.

## Before you apply (Preparation timeline)
The employer must first post the job vacancy on the State Employment Agency (NVA) portal. Once the mandatory posting period is over (often 10–30 days, check official NVA rules), the employer can request an Invitation for you at PMLP. Only after the Invitation is approved can you apply.

<WarningBox>
Your salary must meet the official minimum threshold for foreign workers in Latvia. Check the exact figure on PMLP's website as it changes annually based on the national average wage.
</WarningBox>

## Step-by-Step Application Process

<StepList>
  <StepListItem title="Employer posts vacancy">
    The company posts the job on the NVA portal to prove no local candidate was hired.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Employer requests Invitation">
    The company submits your details to PMLP. PMLP approves the Invitation and generates a number.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Submit Application">
    You take the Invitation Number and your documents (apostilled police clearance, diploma, etc.) to the Latvian embassy or, if allowed, submit them electronically.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Wait for Decision">
    PMLP processes the application. The standard time is 30 days, but can be expedited for an extra fee.
  </StepListItem>
</StepList>

## Fees and Processing Time
* **Processing time:** Standard 30 days. Expedited 10 or 5 days available for an extra fee.
* **State Fee:** Varies. Check the official source: [PMLP State Fees](https://www.pmlp.gov.lv/en)
* **NVA Vacancy Period:** Check the official source: [NVA Rules](https://www.nva.gov.lv)

## After Approval: What to do next
1. Obtain a D-Visa to enter Latvia.
2. Visit PMLP to give [biometrics](./biometrics) and [collect your card](./card-collection).
3. [Declare your address](./address-declaration) and open a local bank account so your employer can pay you.

## Renewal and Changes
If you change employers, your permit is **cancelled**. You must apply for a completely new residence permit with the new employer. Your permit is tied strictly to the company that sponsored you.

## Common Mistakes
* **Applying before the invitation:** You cannot submit your documents until the employer gives you the official Invitation Number from PMLP.
* **Fake job offers:** Be very careful of agents offering "guaranteed" work permits for cash. If you buy a fake job contract, you will be deported and banned from Schengen.

## If your application is refused
Refusals often happen if the employer has tax debts in Latvia or if your qualifications do not match the job description. The employer can appeal the decision.

<FAQ title="What is the EU Blue Card?">
The EU Blue Card is a special work permit for highly qualified workers. It requires a university degree matching the job field and a salary significantly higher than the national average. It offers better mobility rights within the EU.
</FAQ>

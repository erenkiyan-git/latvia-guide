<QuickAnswer>
After living in Latvia legally and continuously for 5 years with a temporary residence permit (*termiņuzturēšanās atļauja*), you can apply for Permanent Residence (*pastāvīgās uzturēšanās atļauja*). This requires passing a basic Latvian language exam (A2 level). Citizenship requires 10 years of residence.
</QuickAnswer>

## Permanent Residence Permit

A Temporary Residence Permit (TRP) must be renewed regularly and is often tied to your job or studies. A Permanent Residence Permit gives you the right to live and work in Latvia indefinitely, without needing sponsorship.

### Requirements for Permanent Residence
To be eligible to apply for permanent residence, you must generally meet these criteria:
1. **5 Years of Continuous Residence:** You must have lived in Latvia on a TRP for at least 5 years. "Continuous" means you have not been outside of Latvia for more than 6 consecutive months, and your total absences over the 5 years do not exceed 10 months. *(Note: Time spent studying on a student permit usually only counts for half).*
2. **Language Requirement:** You must pass the official State Language Examination at the **A2 level** or higher. There are exemptions for people over 75 or those with severe medical conditions.
3. **Financial Means:** You must prove you have a steady, legal income to support yourself.
4. **Clean Record:** No serious criminal record.

### Long-Term EU Resident Status
This is very similar to the Latvian Permanent Residence Permit but comes with an EU-wide status. It allows you slightly easier mobility if you decide to move to another EU country later. The requirements (5 years + language) are essentially the same.

## Latvian Citizenship (Naturalization)

If you want a Latvian passport and the right to vote, you must go through the naturalization process.

### Requirements for Citizenship
1. **10 Years of Residence:** You must have legally lived in Latvia for at least 10 years (typically 5 years on a TRP + 5 years on a Permanent Residence permit).
2. **Language Exam:** You must pass a stricter Latvian language exam.
3. **History and Constitution Exam:** You must pass a test on the basic history, culture, and constitution of Latvia (the Satversme).
4. **National Anthem:** You must know the lyrics to the Latvian national anthem.
5. **Renouncing Previous Citizenship:** Latvia only allows dual citizenship with a specific list of countries (EU, NATO, EFTA, Australia, Brazil, New Zealand). If your country is not on that list, you must renounce your original citizenship to become Latvian.

<FAQ title="Do I have to take the language exam if I studied in Latvian?">
No. If you received your basic or secondary education in the Latvian language, you are exempt from the language exam requirement for both permanent residence and citizenship.
</FAQ>

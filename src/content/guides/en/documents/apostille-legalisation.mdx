<QuickAnswer>
Latvian authorities will not accept a foreign public document (like a birth certificate or police clearance) unless it has an Apostille stamp or has been fully legalised. This stamp proves to Latvia that the document is genuine.
</QuickAnswer>

## What is an Apostille?
An Apostille is an internationally recognized stamp or certificate attached to your original document. It verifies the signature and stamp of the official who issued the document (e.g., the police officer who signed your background check).

## Do I need an Apostille or Full Legalisation?

This depends entirely on where the document was issued:

1. **Issued in an EU/EEA Country:** Under EU regulations, many public documents (like birth or marriage certificates) issued by another EU country **do not** require an Apostille or legalisation to be used in Latvia.
2. **Issued in a country belonging to the Hague Apostille Convention:** (e.g., USA, Turkey, India, UK). You only need a single **Apostille** stamp from that country's designated authority.
3. **Issued in a country outside the Hague Convention:** (e.g., Canada, Nigeria). You must go through **Full Legalisation**. This is a longer process involving the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the issuing country AND the Latvian Embassy.

## How to get an Apostille

You must get the Apostille in the country where the document was issued. **Latvia cannot put an Apostille on a foreign document.**

1. Identify the issuing authority (e.g., the police, a university, a registry office).
2. Find the designated Apostille authority in that country. This is usually the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the Ministry of Justice, or a specific state department (like the Secretary of State in the US).
3. Send the original document to them. They will attach the Apostille.

<WarningBox>
**Do not translate the document before getting the Apostille!** The Apostille stamp itself must also be translated into Latvian. If you translate the document first, the translator's work will be incomplete.
</WarningBox>

## Getting a Latvian Apostille
If you are leaving Latvia and need to use a Latvian document (like a Latvian university diploma) abroad, you can get a Latvian Apostille electronically. Since 2019, sworn notaries in Latvia issue Apostilles in a purely digital format.

<FAQ title="Does the Apostille expire?">
The Apostille itself does not expire. However, the underlying document might (e.g., a police clearance is only valid for 6 months).
</FAQ>

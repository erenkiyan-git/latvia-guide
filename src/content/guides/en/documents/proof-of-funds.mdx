<QuickAnswer>
Latvia requires you to prove you have a place to live and enough money to survive without relying on state welfare. You prove this with a rental contract and a bank statement or an employment contract.
</QuickAnswer>

## 1. Proof of Subsistence (Funds)

Before issuing a visa or residence permit, PMLP wants to see that you have enough money. The minimum amount depends on your reason for moving.

### Minimum Amounts
The required monthly amount is tied to the Latvian minimum wage or average wage and changes every year. 
* **Workers:** Your employment contract must state a gross salary that meets the minimum threshold for foreign workers.
* **Students & Family:** You must show a bank balance covering a specific monthly amount multiplied by 12 months (or the duration of your stay). 
* **Always check the exact current numbers on the PMLP website.**

### How to prove it
* **If you are working:** Your employment contract is your proof.
* **If you are a student or family member:** You must provide an official **Bank Statement**.
  * The account must be in your name (or your sponsor's name).
  * The bank statement must be stamped and signed by the bank.
  * It must show that you possess an active debit/credit card linked to that account.

<WarningBox>
If you use a bank statement from a foreign bank, it must be in English or officially translated. If the currency is not Euros, the balance must clearly be equivalent to the required Euro amount.
</WarningBox>

## 2. Proof of Accommodation

You must prove you have a physical place to live in Latvia.

### Acceptable Documents
* **A Rental Contract:** A signed lease agreement with a landlord in Latvia.
* **A Letter from a University Dormitory:** Confirming you have a reserved bed.
* **A Letter from a Host:** If you are staying with a friend or relative, they must write a letter stating you can live at their address. They must also prove they own the property (e.g., Land Register extract) or have the right to sub-let it.

## 3. Health Insurance

Unless you are employed by a Latvian company (in which case you pay state social taxes and get state healthcare), you must buy private health insurance.
* The policy must cover your entire permitted stay.
* It must guarantee coverage for emergency medical assistance and repatriation (returning your body to your home country in case of death).
* It must be valid in Latvia and the whole Schengen area.

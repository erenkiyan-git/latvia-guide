<QuickAnswer>
If you are a non-EU citizen accepted into a Latvian university, you need a Student Residence Permit (*termiņuzturēšanās atļauja sakarā ar mācībām*). Your university will issue an invitation, and you must prove you have enough funds to support yourself during your studies.
</QuickAnswer>

## Who can apply
* Non-EU citizens who have been officially accepted into an accredited higher education institution in Latvia.
* Exchange students (like Erasmus) staying longer than 90 days.
* Secondary school students (with slightly different requirements regarding guardianship).

**Who cannot apply:** Students doing short-term courses (under 90 days) should use a Schengen Visa instead.

## Before you apply (Preparation timeline)
Start preparing documents as soon as you receive your acceptance letter. Legalising (Apostille) your educational documents and police clearance can take 2-4 weeks in your home country. You cannot apply until your university registers an official Invitation Number at PMLP.

## Step-by-Step Application Process

<StepList>
  <StepListItem title="Get your Invitation Number">
    Pay your tuition fee deposit. The university will then submit an invitation request to PMLP. Once approved, they will email you an Invitation Number.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Prepare and Legalise Documents">
    Get your police clearance and bank statements. If your country is in the Apostille convention, get the police clearance apostilled. Have it translated into Latvian or English.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Submit to the Embassy">
    Book an appointment at the Latvian embassy covering your country. Submit all documents and pay the state fee.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Wait for Decision">
    The embassy sends the file to Riga. PMLP makes a decision and informs the embassy.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Get a D-Visa to Travel">
    If approved, the embassy will stamp a Type-D visa in your passport so you can enter Latvia.
  </StepListItem>
</StepList>

## Fees and Processing Time
* **Processing time:** Standard processing takes up to 30 days. You can pay extra for expedited processing (10 or 5 working days) if the embassy allows it.
* **State Fee:** Check the official source: [PMLP State Fees](https://www.pmlp.gov.lv/en)
* **Proof of Subsistence amount:** Check the official source: [PMLP Subsistence](https://www.pmlp.gov.lv/en)

## After Approval: What to do next
1. Travel to Latvia using your D-Visa.
2. Book an appointment at PMLP to give your [biometrics](./biometrics).
3. [Collect your Residence Permit card](./card-collection).
4. Within 1 month, [declare your address](./address-declaration).

## Renewal and Changes
Student permits are usually issued for the duration of your studies, but the physical card must be renewed every year. You must show a new bank statement and proof of academic progress to renew. If you drop out or are expelled, the university will cancel your invitation and your permit will be revoked.

## Common Mistakes
* **Late police clearance:** Waiting too long to request the police clearance certificate in your home country.
* **Missing Apostille:** Forgetting to get the Apostille on the police clearance.
* **Wrong bank statement:** The bank statement must be in your name, stamped by the bank, and show an active card attached to the account.

## If your application is refused
Common reasons for refusal include fraudulent documents, insufficient funds, or a criminal record. The refusal letter will state the reason. You have the right to appeal the decision within 30 days, as described officially by PMLP.

<FAQ title="Can I work while studying?">
Yes. Full-time students in accredited programs can work up to 20 hours per week during the semester, and up to 40 hours during the summer holidays.
</FAQ>
<FAQ title="Can I stay after graduation?">
Yes, Master's and PhD graduates can usually apply to extend their permit for a few months to look for a job.
</FAQ>

<QuickAnswer>
Latvia offers residence permits for self-employed individuals and investors/business owners. You must submit a detailed business plan, prove sufficient starting capital, and meet annual tax contribution requirements to renew the permit.
</QuickAnswer>

## Who can apply
* Non-EU citizens planning to register as a self-employed person in Latvia.
* Non-EU citizens registering a Limited Liability Company (SIA) or acting as a board member.
* Start-up founders (Latvia has a specific "Startup Visa" route).

**Who cannot apply:** If you just want to freelance for foreign clients without registering a business entity or paying taxes in Latvia, you should look at the [Digital Nomad Visa](./digital-nomad-visa).

## Before you apply (Preparation timeline)
You need to write a solid business plan. The plan must be approved by a sworn auditor before submission. You must also have your starting capital ready in a bank account.

## Step-by-Step Application Process

<StepList>
  <StepListItem title="Prepare your Business Plan">
    Write your business plan and have it verified by an independent sworn auditor in Latvia.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Register the Company (if applicable)">
    If you are opening an SIA, register it with the Enterprise Register (Uzņēmumu reģistrs).
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Submit Application">
    Submit your residence permit application, business plan, and auditor's statement to the Latvian embassy or PMLP.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Wait for Decision">
    PMLP processes the application. Business permits can take longer to evaluate than standard work permits.
  </StepListItem>
</StepList>

## Fees and Processing Time
* **Processing time:** Standard processing takes up to 30 days.
* **State Fee:** Check the official source: [PMLP State Fees](https://www.pmlp.gov.lv/en)
* **Tax Requirements:** Check the official source for the exact amount of taxes you must pay annually to renew the permit.

## After Approval: What to do next
You must enter Latvia, provide biometrics, collect your card, and declare your address. Then, you must immediately begin executing your business plan and paying the required monthly social and income taxes.

## Renewal and Changes
To renew the permit after the first year, PMLP and the State Revenue Service (VID) will check if your business is actually operating. You must prove that your company has paid a specific minimum amount in taxes to the state budget over the year. If you fail to meet this threshold, your permit will not be renewed.

## Common Mistakes
* **Failing the tax threshold:** Underestimating how much revenue you need to generate to cover the mandatory state taxes for renewal.
* **Bad business plan:** Submitting a generic business plan that does not show clear market research for Latvia.

## If your application is refused
Refusals are common if the business plan is deemed unrealistic or if the sworn auditor finds financial discrepancies. You can appeal, but it is often better to submit a new, stronger application.

<FAQ title="What is the Startup Visa?">
The Startup Visa is a fast-track residence permit for founders of innovative technology startups. You must have your startup idea approved by a special government evaluation committee (LIAA) before applying.
</FAQ>

<QuickAnswer>
If PMLP rejects your application, you will receive a decision letter stating the exact legal reason. You have 30 days to appeal the decision in writing to the Head of PMLP.
</QuickAnswer>

## Common Reasons for Refusal

PMLP does not reject applications randomly. Refusals are strictly based on the Immigration Law. Common reasons include:
* **Insufficient Funds:** You could not prove you have the required minimum subsistence.
* **Fake Documents:** Submitting forged diplomas, fake bank statements, or fake hotel bookings. This will also get you banned from the Schengen area.
* **Tax Debts:** Your employer (sponsor) has unpaid taxes in Latvia.
* **Marriages of Convenience:** PMLP interviewed you and your spouse and concluded the marriage was arranged solely for a visa.
* **Threat to Security:** A negative assessment from the Latvian State Security Service (VDD).

## "Request for Additional Information"

Often, before refusing, PMLP will send a letter stating they have paused the processing of your application to request additional documents. 
* **Do not panic.** Read the letter carefully.
* They might ask for a clearer bank statement, an extra translation, or a missing Apostille.
* You are given a strict deadline to provide the missing documents. If you miss the deadline, your application will be refused.

## How to Appeal a Refusal

If you receive a final refusal decision:
1. **Read the Deadline:** You usually have exactly **30 days** from the date of the decision to file an appeal.
2. **Write the Appeal:** The appeal must be written in Latvian. It must clearly state why the official's decision was legally incorrect or provide the missing evidence that caused the refusal.
3. **Submit to the Head of PMLP:** The appeal is reviewed by the Head of the Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs. 
4. **Court:** If the Head of PMLP upholds the refusal, your final option is to take the matter to the Administrative Court of Latvia.

<WarningBox>
**Seek Legal Help.** Appealing a government decision is a legal process. It is highly recommended to hire a licensed, reputable Latvian immigration lawyer to draft your appeal. Do not rely on advice from Facebook groups for a refusal.
</WarningBox>

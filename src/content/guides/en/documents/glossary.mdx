<QuickAnswer>
Official letters in Latvia often use abbreviations. Understanding these common terms will make dealing with the government much easier.
</QuickAnswer>

## Government Institutions

* **PMLP** *(Pilsonības un migrācijas lietu pārvalde)*: The Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs. They handle all visas, passports, and residence permits.
* **VID** *(Valsts ieņēmumu dienests)*: The State Revenue Service. The tax authority in Latvia.
* **NVA** *(Nodarbinātības valsts aģentūra)*: The State Employment Agency. They manage unemployment and must approve foreign worker vacancies.
* **VSAOI** *(Valsts sociālās apdrošināšanas obligātās iemaksas)*: State social insurance mandatory contributions (your social taxes).
* **NVD** *(Nacionālais veselības dienests)*: National Health Service. They manage the state-funded healthcare system.
* **CSDD** *(Ceļu satiksmes drošības direkcija)*: Road Traffic Safety Directorate. They handle driver's licenses and car registrations.

## Everyday Terms

* **Personas kods:** Your Latvian Personal Identification Code. It is a unique number given to you when you receive a residence permit or register as an EU citizen. You need it for almost everything.
* **Deklarēšanās:** The process of officially declaring your living address to the state.
* **Uzturēšanās atļauja:** Residence Permit.
  * *Termiņuzturēšanās atļauja (TRP):* Temporary Residence Permit.
  * *Pastāvīgās uzturēšanās atļauja:* Permanent Residence Permit.
* **E-paraksts:** Electronic signature. The system that allows you to sign documents legally using your ID card or smartphone.
* **E-adrese:** Electronic address. A secure digital mailbox on Latvija.lv where the government sends you official mail.
* **IIN** *(Iedzīvotāju ienākuma nodoklis)*: Personal Income Tax.
* **OCTA:** Mandatory motor third-party liability insurance (car insurance).
* **Komunālie (Komunālie maksājumi):** Utility bills (heating, water, trash, building maintenance).

<QuickAnswer>
Latvia offers specialized residence permits and long-stay visas for researchers, volunteers, religious workers, and investors. These follow similar steps to standard permits but require specific sponsorships.
</QuickAnswer>

## Researchers
Non-EU citizens invited by an accredited Latvian scientific institution can apply for a Researcher Residence Permit.
* **Process:** The scientific institution must sign a hosting agreement with you and submit an invitation to PMLP.
* **Benefits:** Researchers often benefit from easier mobility rules if they need to travel to other EU countries for parts of their research.

## Volunteers and Trainees
If you are coming to Latvia to participate in the European Solidarity Corps or another recognized volunteer program, you can apply for a Long-Stay (Type D) Visa or a Residence Permit.
* **Process:** The host organization in Latvia must submit an invitation. You must prove you have a volunteer agreement and that your accommodation and living expenses are covered by the program.

## Religious Workers
Foreigners arriving for religious activities can obtain a residence permit if invited by a religious organization registered in Latvia. 
* **Process:** The religious organization must be officially registered and must submit the invitation on your behalf.

## Investors (Golden Visa)
Latvia historically offered residence permits to foreigners who purchased real estate or invested heavily in Latvian banks. 
* **Current Status:** The rules for investor permits have become much stricter in recent years, and thresholds have increased significantly. Check the PMLP website for the exact current investment amounts required in real estate, business capital, or state bonds.

<QuickAnswer>
Latvian employers prefer a concise, 1-2 page CV (often using the Europass format or a clean modern design) and a short, highly tailored cover letter. Including a professional photo is common but not legally required.
</QuickAnswer>

## The CV (Curriculum Vitae)

When applying for corporate jobs in Riga, your CV should be in English (unless the job listing is in Latvian). 

* **Format:** Keep it to 1 or 2 pages. The [Europass CV format](https://europa.eu/europass/en/create-europass-cv) is widely recognized and safe, though modern tech companies prefer cleaner, custom designs (like Canva templates).
* **Photo:** Unlike in the UK or US, it is very common and culturally accepted to include a professional headshot on your CV in Latvia. (No selfies or party photos!). However, you cannot be rejected for omitting it.
* **Personal Details:** Include your phone number, email, and LinkedIn URL. Do not include your marital status, religion, or your full personal code (personas kods) on a public CV.
* **Language Skills:** List your languages honestly according to the CEFR framework (A1 to C2). 

<WarningBox>
**Do not lie about your Latvian skills.** If you write "Latvian - Basic", the interviewer will likely test you by asking a question in Latvian. If you cannot answer, you instantly lose trust.
</WarningBox>

## The Cover Letter (Motivācijas vēstule)

Cover letters are still widely expected in Latvia, especially for non-tech roles.

* **Keep it short:** Maximum one page. 3 or 4 paragraphs are enough.
* **Be direct:** Do not use overly emotional or flowery language. Latvians value facts and directness. 
* **Answer the "Why":** Explain why you want this specific job at this specific company, and why you are living in Latvia. Employers often worry that foreigners will leave the country after 6 months, so expressing a commitment to staying in Riga is a huge plus.
* **Format as a formal letter:** Include your contact info at the top, address the hiring manager if you know their name, and sign off professionally.

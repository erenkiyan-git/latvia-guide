You must electronically submit your Tax Booklet (*Algas nodokļu grāmatiņa*) to your employer on your first day so they can calculate your taxes correctly. Every March, you should file an Annual Income Declaration (*Gada ienākumu deklarācija*) to get refunds for medical and education expenses.

## The Electronic Tax Booklet (Algas nodokļa grāmatiņa)

In Latvia, everyone has a digital tax booklet. It tells the State Revenue Service (VID) who your primary employer is. 
If you do not submit this booklet to your employer, they are legally forced to tax your entire salary at the highest possible rate, ignoring your tax-free allowance.

### How to submit it
1. Log into the **VID EDS** (Electronic Declaration System) using your e-Signature or Smart-ID.
2. Go to the "Tax Booklet" (Algas nodokļa grāmatiņa) section.
3. Click "Submit to employer" and select your new company's name from the list.
4. If you have dependents (like minor children), register them here to get an extra tax-free allowance.

## The Annual Income Declaration

The Latvian tax year matches the calendar year. Between **March 1 and June 1**, you should log into the VID EDS system and submit your Annual Income Declaration (Gada ienākumu deklarācija).

The system is highly automated. When you open the form, all your salary and paid taxes are already filled in. You just click "Submit".

### Why you should do it: Tax Refunds!
You can get a percentage of your money back for "Eligible Expenses" (Attaisnotie izdevumi). If you spent money during the year on:
* **Medical bills:** Dentist, doctor visits, hospital stays (but not pharmacy medicines).
* **Education:** University tuition fees.
* **Children:** Kids' educational activities.

**How to claim:** Throughout the year, use the free "Attaisnotie izdevumi" mobile app to take photos of your medical and education receipts and upload them to VID. When March comes, those receipts will automatically appear in your declaration, and the state will refund the overpaid tax directly to your bank account within 3 months.

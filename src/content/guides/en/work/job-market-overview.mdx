<QuickAnswer>
Latvia's job market is heavily concentrated in Riga. The IT, finance, and global business services (customer support) sectors are the biggest employers of English-speaking foreigners. 
</QuickAnswer>

## Key Industries Hiring Foreigners

If you do not speak Latvian, finding a job outside of Riga is extremely difficult. Inside Riga, the market is much more open in specific sectors:

* **IT & Tech:** Software developers, data analysts, and IT support are in high demand. Many tech companies (like Printful, Mikrotik, and various international startups) operate entirely in English internally.
* **Global Business Services (GBS):** Companies like Evolution, SEB, and Circle K have massive shared service centers in Riga. They hire heavily for customer support, content moderation, and back-office roles. If you speak a Nordic language (Swedish, Norwegian, Finnish) or another major European language (German, French) alongside English, you are highly employable here.
* **Finance & Accounting:** International audit firms (PwC, EY, KPMG, Deloitte) and local fintechs often hire international talent.
* **Logistics & Aviation:** AirBaltic (the national airline) is a major employer of foreign pilots and cabin crew, operating largely in English.

## The Language Barrier

The official state language is Latvian. By law, jobs in the public sector and jobs involving direct customer service (e.g., retail, restaurants, medical staff) require a certified level of Latvian. 

Do not expect to get a job in a local shop, cafe, or school if you only speak English. Your focus must be on international corporate offices.

## Salaries
Latvian salaries are generally lower than in Western Europe or Scandinavia, but the cost of living is also lower. The tech sector pays significantly above the national average. 
When discussing salaries in Latvia, always clarify if the number is **Gross (Bruto)** or **Net (Neto)**.

## Work Culture
* **Hierarchical but flatting:** Traditional companies are very hierarchical, but modern startups and IT companies have a flat, informal Scandinavian-style culture.
* **Punctuality:** Being on time is highly valued.
* **Directness:** Latvians tend to communicate directly. They don't use much "small talk" or sugar-coating in a professional environment, which foreigners sometimes mistake for rudeness.

<QuickAnswer>
Your Work Residence Permit is tied to your specific employer. If you quit, your permit becomes invalid. You do not have to leave the country immediately, but your new employer must sponsor you for a new permit.
</QuickAnswer>

## The Rule

Unlike in some countries where a work visa allows you to work anywhere, a standard Latvian Work Residence Permit is strictly linked to the company that sponsored you (the inviter). 

If you resign, or if you are fired:
1. The employer is legally obligated to inform the Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs (PMLP) within 3 days.
2. PMLP will then officially annul your Residence Permit.
3. You will usually be given **30 days** to leave the Schengen area or to submit a new Residence Permit application.

## How to Change Jobs Safely

If you receive a better job offer from another Latvian company, **do not quit your current job until the new company has started the sponsorship process.**

<StepList>
  <StepListItem title="The NVA Vacancy">
    Your new employer must post the job vacancy on the State Employment Agency (NVA) portal. By law, it must stay there for a specific number of days (usually 10 to 30 days, depending on the job category) to prove no local could fill the role.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="The PMLP Invitation">
    Once the NVA period is over, the new employer submits an official invitation (Sponsorship) to PMLP on your behalf.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Submit your Application">
    You submit your application for a new Residence Permit to PMLP. **You can do this while you are still working your notice period at your old job.** 
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="The Gap Period">
    Once your old contract ends and your old permit is annulled, you cannot legally work. If your new permit is still processing, you can legally stay in Latvia while waiting for the decision, but you cannot start the new job yet.
  </StepListItem>
</StepList>

<WarningBox>
**EU Blue Card Holders:** If you hold an EU Blue Card, the rules are slightly more relaxed. You can usually change employers more easily and sometimes just notify PMLP instead of going through the entire NVA vacancy process again. Always verify your specific Blue Card conditions with PMLP.
</WarningBox>

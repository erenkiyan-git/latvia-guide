<QuickAnswer>
Spouses of Latvian citizens, permanent residents, and EU Blue Card holders can work freely. Spouses of standard temporary workers or students usually cannot work unless they find their own employer to sponsor them.
</QuickAnswer>

## Check the ID Card

The easiest way to know if your spouse can work is to look at the back of their Residence Permit (eID) card. 
* If it says **"Tiesības strādāt bez ierobežojumiem"** (Right to employment without restrictions), they can apply for any job immediately.
* If it says nothing about employment, or explicitly forbids it, they cannot legally work.

## Unrestricted Work Rights
Your spouse **has the right** to work freely in Latvia if they received their family reunification permit because you are:
1. A Latvian citizen or non-citizen.
2. A Permanent Resident of Latvia or a Long-Term EU Resident.
3. An **EU Blue Card** holder (highly qualified worker).
4. An intra-corporate transferee.

## Restricted Work Rights
Your spouse **does NOT have the right** to work freely if they received their permit because you are:
1. A standard Temporary Worker (holding a regular Work Residence Permit).
2. A Student.
3. A Digital Nomad.
4. A Business Owner / Self-Employed person on a standard TRP.

<WarningBox>
**What if they want to work?** If a restricted spouse finds a job, their new employer must go through the entire sponsorship process (NVA vacancy posting, PMLP invitation) just like any other foreigner. The spouse must then apply for a new Work Residence Permit based on that specific job.
</WarningBox>

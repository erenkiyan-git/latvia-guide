<QuickAnswer>
If you face illegal treatment at work, you can complain to the State Labour Inspectorate (VDI). They offer free consultations and have the power to fine companies and force them to pay missing wages.
</QuickAnswer>

## Common Violations

Foreigners are sometimes targeted by unethical employers who assume you do not know the local laws. You have the right to complain if your employer:
* Fails to pay your salary on the agreed date.
* Forces you to work unpaid overtime.
* Refuses to grant you your legal paid vacation.
* Pays you "cash in an envelope" to avoid taxes.
* Dismisses you illegally without the 1-month notice period (after probation).
* Fails to provide a safe working environment.

## The State Labour Inspectorate (VDI)

The *Valsts darba inspekcija* (VDI) is the government body responsible for enforcing labor laws. They are generally very helpful and take complaints seriously.

### How to get help:
1. **Free Consultation:** The VDI offers free, anonymous phone consultations. You can call them to explain your situation and ask if your employer's actions are legal.
2. **Filing a formal complaint:** If you want the VDI to investigate your company, you must submit an official written complaint. You can do this electronically via the Latvija.lv portal (using your e-Signature). 

Once a formal complaint is filed, the VDI will inspect the company. If they find violations, they can issue heavy fines and order the employer to rectify the situation (e.g., pay the missing overtime).

## Court 
If the VDI cannot resolve the issue (for example, if the employer disputes the facts of the case), you may have to take the employer to the civil court.

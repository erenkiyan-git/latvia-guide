<QuickAnswer>
Latvian law guarantees 4 calendar weeks of paid holiday per year and extensive sick leave protections. Quitting a job usually requires giving 1 month's notice.
</QuickAnswer>

## Probation Period (Pārbaudes laiks)

When you start a new job, the standard probation period is **3 months** (it cannot be longer by law). 
* During these 3 months, either you or the employer can terminate the employment contract with just **3 days' written notice**.
* You do not need to provide a reason for quitting during probation.

## Paid Annual Leave (Atvaļinājums)

Every employee has the right to paid annual leave.
* **Duration:** You get **4 calendar weeks** (usually 20 working days) of paid holiday per year.
* **Taking leave:** By law, one part of your vacation must be at least 2 uninterrupted calendar weeks. You can split the rest into smaller chunks.
* **Eligibility:** You can ask for your first vacation after working continuously for 6 months at the company.

## Sick Leave (Slimības lapa)

If you get sick, you must visit or call a doctor immediately. Do not just stay home and text your boss. The doctor must open an electronic sick leave certificate (E-veselība system) on the first day of your illness.

* **Sick Leave A (Lapa A):** For the first 9 days of illness, your employer pays your sick pay. They pay 75% of your average earnings for days 2-3, and 80% for days 4-9. (Day 1 is usually unpaid).
* **Sick Leave B (Lapa B):** From the 10th day onwards, the State Social Insurance Agency (VSAA) takes over and pays you sickness benefits (80% of your average earnings).

## Notice Period (Uzmetums / Uzteikums)

If you want to quit your job after your probation period has ended:
* **Standard Notice:** You must give **1 month's written notice** (one month from the date the employer receives your resignation letter).
* **Mutual Agreement:** You and your employer can sign a mutual agreement to terminate the contract earlier (e.g., on the same day or in two weeks) if both parties are happy to do so.

If the employer wants to fire you (and it's not due to gross misconduct), they must also give you 1 month's notice and must follow strict legal procedures to justify the dismissal (e.g., company restructuring).

<FAQ title="What happens to my visa if I quit?">
If you are on a Work Residence Permit sponsored by your employer, quitting your job invalidates your permit. The employer must inform PMLP within 3 days. You usually have 30 days to find a new sponsor and submit a new permit application, or you must leave the country.
</FAQ>

<QuickAnswer>
Latvian employers always negotiate based on Gross (Bruto) salary. About 30% of your Gross salary goes to the state in the form of Social Contributions and Personal Income Tax. The remaining 70% is your Net (Neto) take-home pay.
</QuickAnswer>

## Gross vs. Net

* **Bruto (Gross):** The big number written in your employment contract. This is your total pay *before* taxes.
* **Neto (Net):** The actual amount that arrives in your bank account on payday.

If a job advertisement says "Salary: 2000 EUR", it means Gross. You will take home roughly 1400 EUR. 
*Always use an online "Latvian Salary Calculator" (Algas kalkulators) to check the exact Net amount before accepting a job offer.*

## The Two Main Taxes

Your salary is subject to two major deductions before you see it:

### 1. State Social Insurance Mandatory Contributions (VSAOI)
* **What it is:** This is the social tax. It funds your future pension, sickness benefits, maternity/paternity leave, and unemployment benefits.
* **How much:** The total rate is high (around 34%), but it is split between you and the employer. The employee's share is typically **10.5%** of the gross salary.

### 2. Personal Income Tax (IIN)
* **What it is:** This tax funds the general state and municipal budgets (schools, roads, healthcare).
* **How much:** Latvia has a progressive income tax system. As of the current laws, the rate is **20%** for income up to a certain threshold (covering most average salaries), and **23%** or higher for income above that threshold.

## The Non-Taxable Minimum
To help lower-income workers, Latvia applies a "differentiated non-taxable minimum" (Neapliekamais minimums). This means a portion of your salary is entirely tax-free.
* If your salary is low, a large chunk of it is tax-free.
* If your salary is high (e.g., above 1800 EUR gross), your non-taxable minimum is 0, meaning you pay full tax on every euro.

<WarningBox>
**Submit your Tax Booklet!** To ensure your non-taxable minimum is applied correctly and you aren't overtaxed every month, you must submit your electronic [Tax Booklet](./tax-booklet) to your principal employer via the VID EDS system.
</WarningBox>

Finding a job in Latvia, especially without speaking Latvian, can be challenging but not impossible. The IT and customer service sectors often hire English speakers.

## Top Job Portals
* **CV.lv:** The largest and most popular job portal in Latvia.
* **CVMarket.lv:** Another major job portal.
* **LikeIT.lv:** Specialized portal for IT and tech jobs.
* **NVA.gov.lv (State Employment Agency):** The official government portal. Useful to check official regulations, but less common for English-only corporate jobs.

## Social Media & Networking
* **LinkedIn:** Highly used by recruiters in Riga. Make sure your profile is updated and your location is set to Riga.
* **Facebook Groups:** Search for "Work in Riga" or "Expats in Latvia" groups where jobs are sometimes posted.

<WarningBox>
  Always verify employment contract rules. Do not start working without a signed written contract. If you are a non-EU citizen, you must secure a work permit before starting work.
</WarningBox>

Latvia has over 50 higher education institutions, but international students should stick to state-funded or highly reputable private universities. Always use the official "Study in Latvia" portal to find accredited English-taught programs.

## State vs Private Universities

Latvian universities are broadly split into State (Public) and Private institutions.

**State Universities:**
Generally offer the most prestigious degrees, extensive research facilities, and the largest international student communities.
* *Examples:* University of Latvia (LU), Riga Technical University (RTU), Riga Stradiņš University (RSU).

**Private Universities:**
Can be more specialized and often focus heavily on business, IT, or specific niches. They can be excellent, but you must ensure they are officially accredited.
* *Examples:* Turība University, Stockholm School of Economics in Riga (SSE Riga), Transport and Telecommunication Institute (TSI).

## How to Verify Accreditation

**Never pay an application fee before checking if the university and the specific program are accredited.** 
If you graduate from an unaccredited program, your degree will not be recognized internationally, and you might not qualify for a post-graduation job search visa.

* Use the official [Study in Latvia](https://studyinlatvia.lv) database. Only accredited institutions and programs are listed there.
* Check the State Education Quality Service (IKVD) database if you have doubts.

## Location Matters

While most major universities are in Riga (the capital), there are excellent state universities in other regions:
* **Jelgava:** Latvia University of Life Sciences and Technologies (LBTU) - Great for agriculture, veterinary medicine, and engineering.
* **Liepāja and Ventspils:** Strong regional universities in coastal cities, often with lower living costs than Riga.
* **Valmiera:** Vidzeme University of Applied Sciences.

## Medical Studies
Riga Stradiņš University (RSU) and the University of Latvia (LU) are highly popular among international students (especially from the EU, like Germany and Sweden) for studying Medicine and Dentistry in English. The programs are intensive, highly respected across the EU, and generally more affordable than in Western Europe.

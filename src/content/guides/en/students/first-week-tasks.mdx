<QuickAnswer>
Your first week will be busy. You must finalize your university enrollment, declare your address, get a local SIM card, apply for a transport card, and open a bank account.
</QuickAnswer>

## The Checklist

<StepList>
  <StepListItem title="Get a local SIM card">
    Do this at the airport or any Narvesen kiosk. You need a local phone number immediately to register for everything else. Buy a prepaid card (Zelta Zivtiņa, LMT, or Bite) and top it up with a data package.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Check-in to your Accommodation">
    Get your keys, inspect the room, and get a signed copy of your rental contract or dorm agreement.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="University International Office">
    Go to your university's foreign student department. You must sign the final study agreement and get your student ID card or a certificate proving you are a student.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Declare your Address & Collect your Permit">
    If you are a non-EU citizen, you must go to PMLP to [collect your residence permit card](../documents/card-collection). You should [declare your address](../documents/address-declaration) at the exact same time (bring your rental contract).
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Apply for the e-Talons (Transport Card)">
    Go to a "Rīgas satiksme" customer service center. Bring your passport, your university certificate, and your residence permit. They will take your photo and print a personalized e-ticket giving you the student discount for buses and trams.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Open a Bank Account">
    Many local services (like renting a bike or paying utilities) require a Latvian bank account. Popular choices for students are Swedbank and SEB. You must visit a branch in person with your passport, residence permit, and university acceptance letter.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Download the essential Apps">
    Download Bolt (for taxis and food), Wolt (for food), Mobilly (for train tickets), and the Rīgas satiksme app.
  </StepListItem>
</StepList>

## The Welcome Week
Don't miss the Orientation Week organized by your university or ESN! It is the best time to figure out where your classes are and make your first friends before the hard studying begins.

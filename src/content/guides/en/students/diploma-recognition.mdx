<QuickAnswer>
The Academic Information Centre (AIC) is a government body that evaluates your foreign education documents to ensure they meet Latvian standards. Your university handles this automatically during the application process.
</QuickAnswer>

## What is the AIC?
The Academic Information Centre (AIC) is the Latvian representative to the ENIC-NARIC networks. Their job is to evaluate foreign diplomas, degrees, and transcripts. 

If you are applying to a Latvian university with a high school diploma from India, a bachelor's degree from Nigeria, or even a degree from another EU country, the Latvian university cannot legally enroll you until the AIC confirms your degree is valid.

## How the Process Works
1. **You apply to the university:** You upload your original diplomas and transcripts to the university's application portal.
2. **The University contacts the AIC:** The university submits your documents to the AIC on your behalf. You usually do not need to contact the AIC yourself.
3. **The Evaluation:** The AIC checks their databases, contacts the issuing institution in your home country if necessary, and determines what level of Latvian education your diploma corresponds to.
4. **The Decision:** The AIC issues a formal statement to the university. 

## How long does it take?
The legal timeframe for the AIC to process documents is **1 month**. However, if they need to request additional information from the educational authorities in your home country, the process can be extended up to **4 months**. 

*This is why you must apply to the university as early as possible.*

## What if the AIC rejects my diploma?
If the AIC determines that your high school diploma is not equivalent to a Latvian secondary education (for example, if your home country only requires 10 years of school instead of 12), the university must reject your application. 

## Legalisation / Apostille
The AIC evaluates the *academic* value of the diploma. To prove the document is *legally* genuine, you must still have your diploma [Apostilled or Legalised](../documents/apostille-legalisation) in your home country before sending the hard copies to the university.

<QuickAnswer>
Full-time international students (Bachelor's and Master's) can legally work up to 20 hours per week during the study semester, and up to 40 hours per week during the summer holidays.
</QuickAnswer>

## The 20-Hour Rule

If you hold a valid Student Residence Permit for full-time studies in an accredited program, you automatically have the right to work part-time. 

* **During the semester:** You are limited to **20 hours per week**. This is strictly enforced. Working more than this violates your visa conditions.
* **During the summer holidays:** You are allowed to work full-time (**40 hours per week**).

*Note: If you are an EU/EEA citizen, these hourly restrictions do not apply to you. You have full, unrestricted access to the labor market.*

## What do I need to start working?

1. **A Latvian Bank Account:** Your employer must pay your salary into a bank account. 
2. **Your Personal Code (Personas kods):** Found on your Residence Permit card.
3. **An Employment Contract:** Do not work "cash in hand" (illegally). You must sign a contract.
4. **Tax Booklet (Algas nodokļa grāmatiņa):** You must open this electronically on the State Revenue Service (VID) EDS system to ensure your income taxes are calculated correctly.

## PhD Students
If you are a full-time PhD (Doctoral) student in Latvia, the 20-hour restriction does not apply. You have the right to work full-time (40 hours per week) throughout the year.

## Can I work as a freelancer (Wolt / Bolt)?
Yes, many international students work as food delivery couriers for companies like Wolt or Bolt. However, you must register yourself as a self-employed person (saimnieciskās darbības veicējs) with the State Revenue Service (VID) and pay your own taxes. You are still legally bound by the 20-hour-per-week limit.

<WarningBox>
**Do not prioritize work over studies.** If you fail your exams or stop attending classes because of your job, the university will expel you. If you are expelled, your residence permit will be cancelled, and you will have to leave Latvia immediately.
</WarningBox>

<FAQ title="What happens after I graduate?">
After successfully graduating with a Master's or PhD from a Latvian university, you can usually apply for a special residence permit extension (often up to 9 months) to stay in Latvia and look for a full-time job. Once you find a job, you transition to a standard Work Residence Permit.
</FAQ>

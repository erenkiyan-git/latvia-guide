As a university student in Latvia, you are entitled to many discounts. The most important card you can get is the ISIC (International Student Identity Card).

## How to get an ISIC Card
Most Latvian universities issue ISIC cards directly to their students. 
* Check with your university's student union (Studentu pašpārvalde) or administration office.
* You can also order one online at isic.lv if your university does not issue them directly.

## Where to use it
* **Public Transport:** You can register your ISIC as your e-talons (public transport card) to get the student discount in Riga.
* **Museums & Culture:** Most museums, theaters, and cinemas offer discounted student tickets.
* **Intercity Travel:** Show your card when buying train or bus tickets to other Latvian cities for a discount.

<WarningBox>
  Check the official sources for current discount rates. Do not assume all international student cards will work for local transport discounts unless officially registered.
</WarningBox>

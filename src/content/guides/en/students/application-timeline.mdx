<QuickAnswer>
Start your application 6 to 8 months before the semester begins. You must apply directly to the university, wait for the AIC to recognize your previous diplomas, and then apply for your visa/residence permit.
</QuickAnswer>

## Application Intake Periods

Latvian universities generally have two main intakes:
* **Autumn Intake (September start):** Application deadlines usually range from April to July. This is the main intake for almost all programs.
* **Winter/Spring Intake (February start):** Application deadlines usually range from October to December. Not all programs offer a winter intake.

*Note: Non-EU citizens have much earlier deadlines than EU citizens because the visa and residence permit process takes months.*

## Standard Document Requirements

Every university is different, but you will almost always need:
1. **Previous Education Documents:** High school diploma for Bachelor's; Bachelor's diploma for Master's. These must be original or notarized copies.
2. **Transcripts of Records:** Your grades.
3. **English Proficiency:** IELTS, TOEFL, or sometimes a university-specific English test. (Some exemptions apply if your previous studies were in English).
4. **Passport Copy.**
5. **Application Fee Receipt:** Usually ranging from 100 EUR to 300 EUR (non-refundable).
6. **Motivation Letter / CV.**

## The Timeline

<StepList>
  <StepListItem title="Choose and Apply (6-8 months before)">
    Find your program, submit the online application, and pay the application fee. Upload scans of your documents.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="AIC Recognition (1-2 months)">
    The university will forward your foreign educational documents to the Academic Information Centre (AIC) in Latvia. The AIC verifies that your high school/bachelor degree is genuine and equivalent to the Latvian standard. **You cannot be officially enrolled until the AIC approves your documents.**
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Entrance Exams / Interviews">
    Some programs (especially Medicine, Architecture, or Arts) require passing an online entrance exam or interview.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Conditional Acceptance and Deposit">
    If accepted, you will receive a Conditional Acceptance letter. You must then pay your first semester or first year's tuition fee as a deposit, as well as a security deposit in some cases.
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Visa / Residence Permit (2-3 months)">
    Once the university receives your tuition fee, they will issue the official invitation for your Residence Permit. You then start the [Residence Permit process](../documents/student-permit).
  </StepListItem>
</StepList>

<WarningBox>
**Do not arrive on a Tourist Visa.** Non-EU citizens cannot enter Latvia on a standard Schengen C-Visa (Tourist visa) and then change it to a Student Residence Permit from inside Latvia. You must apply for the residence permit from your home country or a Latvian embassy abroad.
</WarningBox>

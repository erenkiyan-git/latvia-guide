<QuickAnswer>
The biggest mistakes students make are working more than the legal 20-hour limit, forgetting to renew their residence permit on time, and failing classes which leads to expulsion.
</QuickAnswer>

## 1. Violating the 20-Hour Work Limit
As a non-EU full-time student, you are legally restricted to [working 20 hours per week](../working-while-studying) during the semester. 
* **The Mistake:** Working 30 or 40 hours "cash in hand" or trying to juggle two part-time jobs.
* **The Consequence:** The State Revenue Service (VID) and PMLP share databases. If they see you are employed full-time, your residence permit will be cancelled, and you will be deported.

## 2. Missing the Permit Renewal Deadline
Your Student Residence Permit is usually issued for the duration of your studies, but you must register/renew the physical ID card **every year**.
* **The Mistake:** Assuming the card is valid for 4 years without doing anything. Waiting until the card expires to ask for a new one.
* **The Consequence:** You must submit documents (new bank statements, study progress) 30 to 45 days *before* your current card expires. If you miss this, you lose your legal status and must start the entire process again from your home country.

## 3. Ignoring Academic Progress
Your visa is sponsored by the university.
* **The Mistake:** Skipping classes to work, failing exams, and ignoring warnings from the dean's office.
* **The Consequence:** If you do not obtain enough credit points, the university is legally required to expel you. The moment you are expelled, they cancel your PMLP invitation. You usually have 30 days to leave the Schengen area.

## 4. Apartment Deposit Scams
* **The Mistake:** Paying a 500 EUR deposit via bank transfer for an apartment you found on a Facebook group before arriving in Latvia, without ever seeing the apartment or meeting the landlord.
* **The Consequence:** The "landlord" disappears. Always view the apartment and sign a paper contract before transferring large sums of money.

## 5. Forgetting to Cancel Contracts Before Leaving
* **The Mistake:** Finishing your Erasmus semester, packing your bags, and leaving Latvia without officially terminating your internet, phone, and gym contracts.
* **The Consequence:** The companies will continue to bill you. After a few months, they will send the debt to a European debt collection agency. You will face massive fines and a ruined credit rating if you ever return to the EU.

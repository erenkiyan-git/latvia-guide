<QuickAnswer>
Latvia's best attraction is its nature, which is completely free. For kids, the massive Tarzāns adventure park and the Tērvete Nature Park are absolute must-visits.
</QuickAnswer>

## Budget Travel (For Students & Backpackers)

If you are on a tight budget, Latvia is a great country to explore.
* **Free Nature Trails:** The state forestry service (LVM) maintains hundreds of free wooden boardwalk trails through bogs and forests across the country. The Kemeri Bog trail (Ķemeru tīrelis) is stunning at sunrise and completely free.
* **Cheap Transit:** With an [ISIC card](../students/isic-card), you get significant discounts on intercity trains.
* **Hostels:** Riga has a booming hostel scene (e.g., Cinnamon Sally, Tree House) where you can get a bed for 15-20 EUR a night. 

## Family Travel (With Kids)

Latvia is very child-friendly. Almost every town has modern, clean playgrounds.

### Top Destinations for Kids:
1. **Tērvete Nature Park:** A massive, magical forest park filled with wooden sculptures of characters from Latvian fairy tales (witches, dwarves, kings). There are enormous wooden playgrounds and a miniature train.
2. **Līvu Akvaparks:** Located in Jūrmala, this is the largest indoor water park in Northern Europe. Great for cold, rainy days, featuring massive slides and wave pools.
3. **Tarzāns (Sigulda):** The largest open-air adventure park in the Baltics. Kids (and adults) can climb obstacle courses high up in the pine trees.
4. **Riga Zoo (Mežaparks):** A very large, well-maintained zoo located in the beautiful pine forests of Mežaparks in Riga.

<FAQ title="Are there discounts for large families?">
Yes. Latvia has a state "3+ Family Card" (Goda Ģimenes karte) for families with three or more children. It provides massive discounts on train tickets, museum entries, and even discounts at bookstores and pharmacies.
</FAQ>

Latvia is mostly flat, highly forested, and has a beautiful coastline. You can easily escape Riga for a day without a car.

## Jūrmala (The Beach)
Just 30 minutes by train from Riga Central Station. It features white sand beaches, pine forests, and unique wooden architecture.
* **How to get there:** Take the train towards Tukums or Sloka. Get off at **Majori** for the central tourist area.

## Sigulda (The Switzerland of Vidzeme)
About 1 hour and 15 minutes by train. Perfect for autumn walks, exploring medieval castle ruins, and seeing the Gauja River valley.
* **How to get there:** Take the train towards Valmiera or Valga, or take a bus.

## Cēsis
About 2 hours away, this is one of Latvia's oldest and most charming towns, featuring a massive medieval castle and a cozy old town.

*Tip: Buy train tickets via the Mobilly app or at the station.*

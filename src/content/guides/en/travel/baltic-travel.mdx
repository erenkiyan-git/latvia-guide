<QuickAnswer>
Because of the Schengen zone, you can travel freely between Latvia, Lithuania, and Estonia without border checks. The cheapest and most comfortable way is by Lux Express bus.
</QuickAnswer>

## The Schengen Advantage
Latvia, Lithuania, and Estonia are all in the EU and the Schengen Area. 
* If you hold a Latvian Residence Permit or a valid Schengen Visa, you do not need any extra visas to visit the neighboring Baltic states. 
* There are no physical border checkpoints. You just drive across the border. (However, always carry your passport and residence permit, as random police checks can happen).

## Traveling to Estonia (Tallinn & Tartu)

* **Bus (The best way):** Companies like *Lux Express* and *Ecolines* run buses almost every hour from Riga to Tallinn. The trip takes about 4.5 hours. The buses are incredibly comfortable, with free Wi-Fi, movies, and hot coffee machines onboard. 
* **Train:** There is a train route connecting Riga to Tartu, the vibrant student city in southern Estonia.
* **Ferry:** Once you reach Tallinn, you can take a 2-hour ferry across the sea directly to Helsinki, Finland.

## Traveling to Lithuania (Vilnius & Kaunas)

* **Train:** A modern train route (operated by LTG Link) now connects Riga directly to Vilnius. It takes just over 4 hours.
* **Bus:** Again, Lux Express is the standard. You can reach Vilnius or Kaunas in about 4 hours. 
* **Car:** Driving south to Lithuania is very easy. The roads are good, and you can stop at the famous Hill of Crosses (Kryžių kalnas) just across the border near Šiauliai.

## AirBaltic
If you want to travel further (to Scandinavia, Western Europe, or the Middle East), Riga Airport is the hub for *airBaltic*, the national airline. They offer direct, affordable flights to over 70 destinations across Europe.

Visit Jūrmala in July for the beach, Sigulda in October for the stunning autumn leaves, and Kuldīga in spring to see the flying fish.

## Summer (June to August)
Summer is spectacular. The days are incredibly long (it is still light outside at 11:00 PM), and the whole country moves to the coast or the countryside.
* **Jūrmala & Liepāja:** The two best beach destinations. Jūrmala is close and trendy; Liepāja is further away but famous for its white sand, windsurfing, and music festivals.
* **Latgale:** The eastern region of Latvia, known as the "Land of Blue Lakes." Perfect for renting a cabin, kayaking, and experiencing traditional pottery and Catholic festivals.

## Autumn (September to November)
Autumn in Latvia is short but intensely beautiful, followed by a dark, wet November.
* **Sigulda & Gauja National Park:** The absolute best place to be in October. The entire river valley turns bright gold and red. You can hike, take the cable car over the river, or visit Turaida Castle.
* **Mushroom Picking:** A national obsession. In September, Latvians go deep into the forests to pick wild mushrooms. (Do not do this unless you go with a local who knows which ones are poisonous!).

## Winter (December to March)
The sun disappears, and the snow arrives.
* **Christmas Markets:** The Old Town in Riga hosts beautiful, cozy Christmas markets serving hot mulled wine (Karstvīns) and roasted almonds.
* **Skiing:** Latvia is flat, so there are no massive mountains, but there are decent ski hills like *Žagarkalns* (in Cēsis) and *Riekstukalns* for snowboarding and skiing. Cross-country skiing in the forests is also hugely popular.

## Spring (April to May)
The snow melts, leaving everything muddy until late April, when the country suddenly turns bright green.
* **Kuldīga:** The most beautiful town in the west (Kurzeme). Visit in late April or early May to see the "Flying Fish." Vimba fish actually jump up the Ventas Rumba (the widest waterfall in Europe) as they swim upstream to spawn.

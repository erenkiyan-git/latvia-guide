<QuickAnswer>
Tourists often get fined for drinking alcohol on the street, taking unlicensed taxis outside nightclubs, or forgetting to validate their public transport tickets.
</QuickAnswer>

## 1. Drinking Alcohol on the Street
It is strictly illegal to consume alcohol in public places (streets, parks, beaches) in Latvia.
* **The Consequence:** The Municipal Police (Pašvaldības policija) patrol the Old Town heavily. If they catch you walking with an open beer can, they will fine you on the spot (usually around 15 - 50 EUR). Keep the drinking inside bars or on licensed terraces.

## 2. The Unbranded Taxi Scam
After a night out in Vecrīga (Old Town), tourists often jump into the first taxi they see. 
* **The Consequence:** Unbranded or "private" taxis will turn on a predatory night meter and charge you 50 EUR for a 5-minute drive back to your hotel. If you argue, they lock the doors. 
* **The Fix:** Always use the Bolt app, or find an official branded Baltic Taxi (green) or Red Cab.

## 3. Forgetting to "Tap" the E-Talons
Tourists often buy a cardboard public transport ticket (e-talons), walk onto the tram, sit down, and assume the ticket is active.
* **The Consequence:** You must physically tap the ticket against the yellow card reader *every single time* you board a vehicle. If ticket inspectors board the tram and see you haven't validated it, they will fine you.

## 4. Being Too Loud
Latvians are generally quiet and reserved in public spaces. 
* **The Consequence:** Large tourist groups (especially stag dos/bachelor parties) shouting on the streets or being aggressively loud in restaurants are deeply disliked by the locals. You will not get good service if you are causing a scene.

<WarningBox>
**The "Friendly Local" Bar Scam:** If a beautiful local woman approaches you on the street in the Old Town and asks you to buy her a drink at a "great bar she knows nearby," politely decline and walk away. This is a classic scam. The bar will charge you hundreds of euros for a single cheap drink, and large bouncers will force you to pay before you can leave.
</WarningBox>

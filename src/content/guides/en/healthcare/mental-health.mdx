## Finding a Therapist or Psychologist

State-funded psychological help is very limited and almost exclusively in Latvian. Most expats use private practitioners.

* **How to find one:** There are several private centers (like *Skalbes* or various private psychology practices in the city center) that employ English-speaking therapists. 
* **Cost:** Expect to pay between 40 and 70 EUR per 50-minute session.
* **Insurance:** Check your [employer's private health insurance](../health-insurance). Many "Gold" level policies now cover 5 to 10 sessions with a certified psychologist per year.

## Psychiatrists and Medication

If you need a diagnosis (e.g., for ADHD, severe depression) or prescription medication, a psychologist cannot help you. You must see a Psychiatrist (Psihiatrs).
* You can book a private appointment with a psychiatrist at major clinics like ARS or VC4. 
* They can issue digital e-prescriptions that you can use at any pharmacy.

<WarningBox>
**ADHD Medication Warning:** Latvia has very strict laws regarding stimulants (like Adderall or Concerta). Adderall is entirely illegal and unavailable. Other medications are heavily controlled. If you are moving to Latvia with a prior ADHD diagnosis, bring copies of your medical records and prepare to undergo a re-evaluation by a Latvian psychiatrist before they will prescribe local equivalents.
</WarningBox>

## Emergency Crisis Lines

If you or someone you know is in severe distress or experiencing suicidal thoughts, please reach out:
* **Skalbes Crisis Hotline:** Call **119** or **27722292**. They offer psychological crisis counseling. They have English-speaking volunteers available.
* **General Emergency:** Call **112** if there is an immediate risk to life.

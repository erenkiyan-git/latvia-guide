<QuickAnswer>
Dental care in Latvia is fully privatized for adults. It is famous for being incredibly high quality while remaining significantly cheaper than in the UK, USA, or Scandinavia.
</QuickAnswer>

## The Quality and Cost

Latvia has a booming "medical tourism" sector specifically for dentistry. The clinics are equipped with state-of-the-art technology, and the dentists are highly trained.

Because it is fully private, you do not have to wait. You can usually get an appointment for a cleaning or a filling within a few days. 
* **Dental Hygiene (Cleaning):** 50 - 70 EUR.
* **Standard Filling:** 60 - 90 EUR.
* **Root Canal:** 150 - 300 EUR (depending on complexity).

## Finding a Dentist
There are hundreds of dental clinics in Riga. 
* **Major Dental Centers:** Stomatoloģijas institūts (The Institute of Stomatology) is the absolute highest authority for complex surgeries or emergencies. 
* **Private Clinics:** Clinics like *Adenta*, *Dental Art*, or the dental departments in *ARS* are very popular with expats and offer full services in English.

## Children's Dentistry
Unlike adults, dental care for children (up to the age of 18) is **fully paid for by the state**.
However, just like the rest of the state healthcare system, there are massive waiting lists. Many parents choose to pay for a private dentist for their children to avoid waiting 6 months for a simple filling.

<FAQ title="Does my health insurance cover the dentist?">
It depends entirely on your employer's specific policy. Basic policies cover nothing. Good corporate policies often have a "Dental Limit" (e.g., they will cover 50% of your dental bills up to a maximum of 200 EUR per year). Always check your insurance app before you go.
</FAQ>

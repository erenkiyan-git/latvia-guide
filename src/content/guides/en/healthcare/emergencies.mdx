If you experience a medical emergency in Latvia, help is available.

## Emergency Number: 112
Call **112** for all major emergencies (Ambulance, Police, Fire). The operators speak Latvian, Russian, and English.

## 24/7 Medical Advice Line
If you have a non-life-threatening medical issue outside of normal clinic hours, you can call the state family doctor advisory phone: **66016001**.
* Available 5:00 PM to 8:00 AM on weekdays.
* 24 hours on weekends and public holidays.
* Consultations are mostly in Latvian and Russian.

## Going to the Hospital (Traumpunkts)
For injuries (cuts, broken bones), you can go to the trauma point of a major hospital:
* **Riga East University Hospital (Gaiļezers)** - Hipokrāta iela 2
* **Pauls Stradiņš Clinical University Hospital** - Pilsoņu iela 13

<WarningBox>
  If you do not have a European Health Insurance Card (EHIC) or private health insurance, emergency hospital visits and ambulance call-outs can be very expensive.
</WarningBox>

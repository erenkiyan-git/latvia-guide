<QuickAnswer>
Your Family Doctor (Ģimenes ārsts) is your gateway to the Latvian healthcare system. You must register with one to get cheap state-funded care, official sick leave, and referrals to specialists.
</QuickAnswer>

## The Role of the Family Doctor

In Latvia, you do not just walk into a hospital or call a heart surgeon if you feel ill. 
You go to your Family Doctor. They treat basic illnesses, prescribe medication, open your [official sick leave](../work/probation-leave), and—crucially—write you a **referral (nosūtījums)**. 
*Without a referral from your Family Doctor, you cannot access cheap state-funded specialists; you will have to pay full private prices.*

## How to Register

If you are eligible for state-funded healthcare (e.g., you are an employed foreigner paying taxes), you have the right to register with a state-contracted doctor.

<StepList>
  <StepListItem title="Find a doctor in your area">
    Family doctors operate on a territorial basis. They must accept you if your declared address is in their specific catchment area. You can call the National Health Service (NVD) at 80001234 (they speak English) and ask: "Which doctors serve my address?"
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Call the Clinic">
    Call the doctor's practice and ask if they are taking new patients and if the doctor speaks English. (This is the hardest part; many older doctors only speak Latvian and Russian, and many have full patient lists).
  </StepListItem>
  <StepListItem title="Sign the Agreement">
    Visit the clinic, show your ID card, and sign a registration agreement. You are now their patient.
  </StepListItem>
</StepList>

## The Private Alternative

If you cannot find an English-speaking state doctor, or if you are a non-EU student (who is not eligible for state care), you must register with a Private Family Doctor or General Practitioner at a major private clinic (like ARS, Veselības Centrs 4, or MFD). 
* **The Catch:** A visit to a private GP costs around 40-60 EUR (instead of the 2 EUR state fee), but you can usually get an appointment on the same day.

<WarningBox>
**Do not wait until you are sick.** It can take weeks to find a Family Doctor, sign the paperwork, and get your first appointment. Start this process the month you arrive in Latvia.
</WarningBox>

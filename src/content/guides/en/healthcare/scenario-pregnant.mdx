<QuickAnswer>
Maternity care in state hospitals is completely free for residents paying social tax. However, a baby born in Latvia to two foreign parents does NOT automatically become a Latvian citizen.
</QuickAnswer>

## Maternity Care and Birth

If you are a foreigner formally employed in Latvia (paying social taxes/VSAOI) or the spouse of one, pregnancy and childbirth care is highly subsidized or entirely free.

* **State Hospitals:** The most popular place to give birth is the Riga Maternity Hospital (Rīgas Dzemdību nams). The standard delivery, midwife assistance, and a shared room are free. 
* **Paid Upgrades:** Many parents choose to pay for "extras," such as a private family room (where the father can sleep), an epidural (if not medically strictly necessary but requested), or to have a specific doctor of their choice present at the birth.
* **Private Clinics:** There are also private maternity clinics (like Jūrmala Hospital or Sigulda Hospital, which are famously popular with expats) where you pay out of pocket for a more "hotel-like" experience.

## Citizenship and Visas for the Baby

Latvia does **NOT** have birthright citizenship (Jus soli). Just because your baby is born on Latvian soil does not make them Latvian.

* **The Rule:** If neither parent is a Latvian citizen, the baby inherits the citizenship of the parents.
* **The Paperwork:** 
  1. The hospital gives you a birth certificate.
  2. You must take this to your home country's embassy in Riga (or nearest location) to register the baby and get them a passport.
  3. Once the baby has a passport, you must apply to PMLP for the baby's Latvian Residence Permit (Family Reunification).

## Maternity and Paternity Leave

Latvia has some of the most generous parental leave laws in the world.
* **Maternity Leave (Mātes alga):** Paid time off for the mother immediately before and after the birth.
* **Paternity Leave:** The father is entitled to 10 working days of paid leave within the first 6 months of the child's life.
* **Parental Leave (Bērna kopšanas atvaļinājums):** One parent can take paid leave to stay home with the child until they are 1 or 1.5 years old. The state pays a percentage of your previous salary (funded by your VSAOI contributions).

<FAQ title="What if my contract ends while I am pregnant?">
If your work contract ends, your employer will cancel your residence permit sponsorship. This is a highly stressful situation for non-EU citizens, as you may lose your legal right to stay in the country. You must either find a new employer to sponsor you, switch to a family permit (if your spouse works), or contact PMLP immediately to discuss your specific legal options.
</FAQ>

<QuickAnswer>
Private health insurance is essential to bypass long state waiting lists. The best policies are provided as a perk by your employer. Non-EU foreigners must also buy a basic policy just to get their visa.
</QuickAnswer>

## 1. The Mandatory Immigration Insurance
If you are a non-EU citizen applying for any visa or residence permit (Student, Work, Nomad), PMLP requires you to have a basic Latvian health insurance policy (Veselības apdrošināšana ārzemniekiem). 
* **What it covers:** Almost nothing. It only covers emergency life-saving hospitalizations or repatriation in case of death. It does not cover dentistry, check-ups, or a sore throat.
* **Cost:** Very cheap, around 50 - 70 EUR for the whole year. You buy it online from companies like BTA, Balta, or Ergo.

## 2. Employer-Provided Insurance (The Good Stuff)

Because state healthcare [waiting lines are so long](./healthcare-system), almost all decent corporate employers in Latvia provide their staff with a comprehensive Private Health Insurance card (Veselības apdrošināšanas polise). 

**How it works:**
* You receive a plastic card or digital app from the insurance company.
* When you go to a private clinic (e.g., for an X-ray, a dermatologist, or a dentist), you hand them the card.
* The clinic charges the insurance company directly, or you pay upfront and upload the receipt to the insurance app to get refunded within a few days.

**Levels of Coverage:**
Employers usually buy different "levels" of insurance (Bronze, Silver, Gold). A good Gold policy will cover:
* 100% of the cost of private specialist visits.
* Paid vaccinations.
* 50% to 75% of dental work.
* Sports club/gym memberships (sometimes).

<FAQ title="Can I buy good private insurance myself?">
Yes, but it is expensive. Insurance companies prefer selling massive group policies to corporations. If you try to buy a comprehensive policy as an individual, it will cost several hundred euros a year, and they will usually refuse to cover any pre-existing medical conditions.
</FAQ>

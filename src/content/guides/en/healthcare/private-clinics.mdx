If you have employer health insurance (or can afford to pay 40-80 EUR out of pocket), you should skip the state queues and book an appointment directly with a specialist at a major private clinic like ARS or Veselības Centrs 4.
</QuickAnswer>

## The Major Private Clinics in Riga

Latvia's private healthcare is centralized in massive "polyclinics". These are large medical centers that house hundreds of doctors across every specialty under one roof. They have the newest MRI and X-ray machines, and the facilities look more like modern hotels than hospitals.

The biggest networks are:
1. **Medicīnas Sabiedrība ARS:** Located in the very center of Riga. Highly reputable, massive staff, and very accustomed to treating foreign expats and diplomats.
2. **Veselības Centrs 4 (VC4):** The largest private network in Latvia with many branches across Riga. Excellent for diagnostics and specialist appointments.
3. **Veselības centru apvienība (VCA):** Another very large network of polyclinics.
4. **Diplomatic Dental & Medical Centre:** Specifically targets the expat community. Excellent English, but higher prices.

## How to Book an Appointment
You do not need a referral from a Family Doctor if you are paying privately. 

You can simply call the clinic's reception or use their online booking systems (like piearsta.lv) and say: "I want to see a cardiologist next week, and I am paying privately." 

## Language Barrier
Almost all doctors in the private sector speak English and Russian alongside Latvian. Medical tourism is a large industry in Latvia (especially for dentistry, dermatology, and vein surgeries), so the staff is highly trained to handle international patients.

<QuickAnswer>
If you are an EU/EEA citizen, your EHIC card gives you access to state-funded emergency and necessary healthcare in Latvia exactly as if you were a local.
</QuickAnswer>

## What is the EHIC?
The European Health Insurance Card (EHIC / EVAK in Latvian) is a free card that proves you are insured in another EU country (e.g., Germany, Spain, Sweden). 

## Using it in Latvia
If you are an Erasmus student, a tourist, or a temporary worker in Latvia, and you get sick:
1. Go to a hospital or a state-contracted clinic.
2. Show them your EHIC card and your passport.
3. You will receive necessary medical treatment (e.g., treating a broken leg, an asthma attack, or an urgent infection) under the same conditions and at the same cost (just the tiny patient co-payment) as people insured in Latvia.

<WarningBox>
**What it does NOT cover:** The EHIC is not a substitute for travel insurance. It does not cover private healthcare (which most people in Latvia use for speed), it does not cover planned surgeries, and it does not cover the cost of flying you back to your home country if you are severely injured.
</WarningBox>

## Getting a Latvian EHIC
If you are a foreigner formally employed in Latvia (paying Latvian social taxes), you are officially part of the Latvian healthcare system. You can request a Latvian EHIC card from the National Health Service (NVD). You should carry this card when you travel to other EU countries on holiday.

<QuickAnswer>
Latvia has a mixed system. The state provides heavily subsidized healthcare (NVD) for citizens, permanent residents, and employed foreigners paying social tax. However, waiting times for state specialists are often months long, so many people use private clinics.
</QuickAnswer>

## 1. State-Funded Healthcare (Valsts apmaksāta medicīna)

If you have the right to state-funded healthcare, you do not get medicine and doctors for completely free. Instead, the state pays the bulk of the cost, and you pay a small co-payment (Pacienta iemaksa).

* **Example:** A visit to your Family Doctor might cost you 2 EUR (the co-payment). A night in a state hospital might cost you 10 EUR. 
* **The Problem:** The state healthcare budget is limited. Every clinic gets a "quota" (kvota) of state money. When the quota for the month runs out, you either have to wait until next month/year, or you have to pay the full private price for the service. Waiting times for state-funded MRI scans or specialist visits can sometimes be 6 months.

### Who is eligible?
You have access to state-funded healthcare if you are:
* A Latvian citizen or non-citizen.
* A foreigner with a Permanent Residence Permit.
* A foreigner with a Temporary Residence Permit who is **formally employed** in Latvia and paying Social Contributions (VSAOI).
* An EU citizen holding an EHIC card or S1 form.

*Note: If you are an international student from outside the EU, you do NOT have access to state-funded healthcare. You must use private clinics and your private health insurance.*

## 2. Private Healthcare (Maksas medicīna)

Because state queues are so long, the private healthcare sector in Latvia is massive, modern, and excellent. 

* **The Benefit:** If you need to see a dermatologist or get an ultrasound, you can usually book an appointment at a private clinic for the very next day. 
* **The Cost:** You pay the full price out of pocket. A specialist consultation is usually between 40 and 80 EUR. An MRI might be 150 - 250 EUR.
* **The Solution:** Most employed people rely on their employer-provided Private Health Insurance to cover these costs.

<FAQ title="What is the E-veselība system?">
E-veselība (E-health) is the national digital health database. It stores all your medical records, sick leave certificates, and digital prescriptions. You can log into it using your Latvian eID card or Smart-ID.
</FAQ>

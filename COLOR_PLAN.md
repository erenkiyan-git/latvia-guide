# Color Plan & Options (Revised)

Here are three palette options designed to match your revised goal: a refined cool-gray palette with the Latvian carmine red acting as a strong, distinct brand identity.

You can preview all three options live by visiting: **[http://localhost:4321/palette-preview](http://localhost:4321/palette-preview)**

---

## Option A: Gray + Authentic Carmine
This option uses the exact deep, wine-toned carmine of the Latvian flag for the primary interactive elements, set against a highly professional cool-gray canvas.
*   **Page Background:** Cool light gray (`#EDEFF2`)
*   **Section Bands:** Soft cool gray (`#E1E5EA`)
*   **Primary (Carmine):** 50: `#F3E1E3`, 500: `#B32A35`, 600: `#9E3039` (Buttons), 700: `#7F232B` (Hover), 900: `#4A1519`
*   **Text:** Deep slate (`#1B2027` for headings, `#2A3039` for body)
*   **Footer:** Dark anchoring slate (`#2E343D`)

## Option B: Gray + Warm Carmine
A slight variation on Option A. The primary accent is still wine-toned carmine, but pushed just a bit warmer and lighter to ensure primary buttons pop even more against the cool grays, while avoiding bright "error" red.
*   **Page Background:** Cool light gray (`#EDEFF2`)
*   **Primary (Warm Carmine):** 600: `#AD2B36` (Buttons), 700: `#8E222A` (Hover)
*   **Other colors:** Same as Option A.

## Option C: Gray + Teal (For Comparison)
This shows the previous teal direction, but updated to use the new refined cool-gray background instead of the warmer stone/sand background, allowing you to directly compare the vibe.
*   **Page Background:** Cool light gray (`#EDEFF2`)
*   **Primary (Teal):** 600: `#0D9488` (Buttons), 700: `#0F766E` (Hover)
*   **Other colors:** Same as Option A.

---

### My Recommendation
**(Recommended) Option A: Gray + Authentic Carmine**  
This creates the strongest, most authentic identity for a Latvia-focused guide. The deep wine-toned carmine (`#9E3039`) provides excellent contrast against the cool grays without ever being confused for a bright "danger" red, resulting in a highly distinguished, official look.
